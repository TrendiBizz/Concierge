Easiest: add the Concierge bookmark to your Home Screen

Pictures are real iPhone screens (steps 2 and 10 are drawings). Yours may look slightly different. Tap the light-blue outline.

This adds a bookmark (a shortcut icon) to your Home Screen. It is not an app download. Ten short steps, about a minute. Do this in Safari. Step 9 is the last thing to do. Stop there.

  1. 1Tap the purple button below.
    Download Concierge icon
  2. 2Tap Allow when iPhone asks “This website is trying to download a configuration profile. Do you want to allow this?”
  3. 3Tap Close on “Profile Downloaded”.
  4. 4Open the Settings app (the grey gear). Tap Profile Downloaded near the top.Can’t see it? Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap Concierge.
  5. 5Tap Install (top right).It says “Not Signed” in red. That is normal: we do not pay Apple for a signing certificate. The profile only adds the icon and changes nothing else.
  6. 6Tap Install again on the “Unsigned Profile” warning.
  7. 7Type your phone passcode when iPhone asks.
  8. 8Tap Install in the pop-up.
  9. 9Tap the blue tick (top right). STOP. The bookmark is installed. You are done.iPhone then leaves you in Settings on a “VPN & Device Management” or “Profile” page. That is only Apple’s list of what is installed. Do nothing there, and never tap Remove Profile (it deletes the bookmark). Swipe up from the bottom edge to leave Settings.
  10. 10Later, to use Concierge: tap the purple Concierge bell bookmark on your Home Screen. This is not part of the install.The app opens full screen. If you scanned the QR from inside your Studio you are already signed in; otherwise it asks for a six-digit code we email you.

To remove the bookmark later: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Concierge → Remove Profile.

Prefer not to use a profile? Add it from the Share menu instead
This is the Share button — in Safari it is in the bottom bar (top right on iPad). On newer iOS it can be under the ⋯ button instead.
  1. 1Tap the Share button — the square with the arrow, in the bottom bar on iPhone (top right on iPad). If you cannot see the bar, swipe up on the page once.
  2. 2Scroll the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add (top right).

You should see: a purple bell icon named “Concierge” on your Home Screen. Tap it to open the app.

No Share button anywhere on screen? If the address bar has no toolbar at all, you are in a link preview inside another app. Tap ⋯ and choose Open in Safari, or open Safari and paste this address: concierge.trendibizz.com/studio/install
