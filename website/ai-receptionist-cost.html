WHAT THE PRICE COVERS

One monthly subscription, not a per-call charge

The assistant

Answers from your own website and business information, captures contact details and records every enquiry in the built-in CRM.

Bookings and follow-up

Agreed appointment times, booking records and follow-up tasks, kept with the enquiry.

Owner notifications

New-enquiry alerts to the email and phone you nominate, with your replies routed back into your own CRM.

Setup and testing

Your website is imported into its knowledge, and each channel is configured and tested before activation.

Website chat

One line of code adds the chat window. This is the channel that needs no extra provider.

Portal access

A private workspace for your team, with owner, editor and viewer roles.

WHAT CHANGES THE COST

What sits outside the Trendi subscription

  • Telephone provider and number — a business number and voice provider are needed for calls.
  • SMS provider or carrier charges for text messages.
  • Email hosting and domain costs if you do not already have them.
  • Any paid third-party CRM or calendar integration you choose to connect.

Provider prices vary by country and are not controlled by Trendi. We agree what is needed before you start and test each connection.

COMPARED

AI receptionist cost compared with a human answering service

Fixed vs per call

Trendi is a fixed monthly subscription. Human answering services usually charge per call, per minute or per agent hour, which makes busy months more expensive.

Coverage

A fixed subscription covers the channels you enable without a per-contact fee. A human service may charge for every call or message handled.

Complex work

People are better at negotiation and difficult calls. Budget for human handover where it matters; Concierge is designed to route those conversations to your team.

Compare the total cost, including provider charges and the human time you keep, not just the headline fee.

COST QUESTIONS

What people ask about price

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

Trendi Concierge is USD $400 a month with a 3-month minimum, then it renews monthly. After the first three months you can move to a yearly plan at USD $4,080. The subscription covers the Concierge service; separate provider costs for a phone number or SMS delivery may apply.

Is there a setup fee?

No separate Trendi setup fee is listed. Setup, knowledge import and channel testing are part of onboarding. Provider charges from a telephone or messaging company are separate and depend on the provider.

Is it charged per call or per minute?

No. The Trendi subscription is monthly, not per call or per minute. Usage limits and fair-use rules are agreed in the service terms.

What is included in the price?

The Concierge assistant, knowledge loaded from your website, website chat, enquiry capture, booking, the built-in CRM, follow-up tracking and owner notifications. Phone, email and SMS channels are enabled after provider setup and testing.

Does it include a phone number?

No. A business phone number and voice provider are required for telephone answering, and are arranged separately. Website chat works without a number.

Are there hidden costs?

We list the Trendi subscription and say plainly that provider charges are separate. Local taxes may apply depending on your location. We do not promise leads or sales.
