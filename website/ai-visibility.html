What AI visibility means: Indexed, Found, Mentioned

AI visibility is the chance your business gets read, understood and named when someone asks an AI tool a question. It works in three steps: Indexed (crawlers can fetch the page), Found (pages match real buyer searches), then Mentioned (an answer names you). A mention is not a link, and none of the three is guaranteed.

1. Indexed. Crawlers can fetch your pages and read them. Pages load. They aren't blocked by robots.txt or a stray noindex tag. The important content sits in text, not hidden in images or scripts. If a crawler can't read the page, nothing else matters.

2. Found. Your pages match the searches real buyers make in Google and Bing. AI tools often draw on web search, so clear pages about what you sell, who it's for and where you work still do most of the work.

3. Mentioned. Once you're indexed and found, you have a chance of being named in AI answers. It's a chance, not a guarantee. We can improve the conditions, but we don't control what an AI tool says.

A mention is not a link

These are two different outcomes. Keep them apart.

  • A mention is when an AI answer names your business, in text, as part of its response.
  • A link is when the answer points to your website, so a reader can click through.

You can be mentioned without being linked. Someone may read your name and never visit your site, or they may search for you later. You can also be linked without being named prominently. Your page appears as a source, but the answer doesn't recommend you.

Both matter, and they tell you different things. A mention means people may be hearing about you. A link shows where traffic could come from. Reporting only one gives you half the picture, so we track both and report them separately.

Pew Research Center (22 Jul 2025) found that Google users who saw an AI summary clicked a traditional search result in 8% of visits, against 15% without one, and clicked a link inside the summary in 1% of visits. So Trendi counts a name in an answer and a link to your site as separate outcomes.

ChatGPT vs Perplexity: how mentions and links differ

In our observation around Oct 2026, the two behave differently:

  • ChatGPT answers may name a business without linking to its website.
  • Perplexity answers may include source links. Check whether a link actually supports a mention.

So the same piece of work can show up differently depending on the tool. A business might be named in ChatGPT with no click available, and cited with a link in Perplexity. Neither is automatically better. They are different outcomes.

These behaviours change as the products change, sometimes without notice. Treat this section as a snapshot, not a rule. That is one reason we measure rather than assume (see below).

Is this still SEO? What Google's guidance says

Mostly, yes. Google's guidance says the fundamentals still apply to its AI features. No special trick replaces good, crawlable, useful content. Read it for yourself:

  • AI optimisation guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
  • AI features and your website: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
  • Succeeding in AI search: https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/05/succeeding-in-ai-search

The practical takeaway: pages need to be indexable, helpful and clearly written for people. That covers the Indexed and Found steps. We do that work, then check what shows up in AI answers.

Ahrefs (4 Feb 2026) found that the presence of an AI Overview correlated with a 58% lower average click-through rate for the top-ranking page, in a study of 300,000 keywords on desktop comparing Dec 2025 with Dec 2023. A high ranking can sit alongside fewer clicks, which is why Trendi tracks mentions and links as well as rankings.

Google isn't the only engine, and other tools have their own behaviour. Where we say something about them, it comes from what we've observed, not from published guarantees.

Crawl readiness: IndexNow and llms.txt

Two small technical items come up a lot. Here is what each one does, and what it doesn't.

IndexNow tells participating search engines when your pages have been added, changed or removed, so they can recrawl sooner. Bing and its partners support it. Google does not use IndexNow, so don't expect it to notify Google. Documentation: https://www.indexnow.org/documentation

llms.txt is a proposed plain-text file that aims to give AI tools a tidy, readable overview of your site. Treat it as a crawl-readiness aid only. It helps make your content easy to read. Support varies by tool. It isn't a ranking signal, and it doesn't make anyone mention you. Proposal: https://llmstxt.org/ and our own explainer at llms.txt.

Both fit into getting Indexed. Neither is a shortcut to being Mentioned.

What an AI visibility audit checks

Each audit reports what we observed and when. Mentions and links are logged separately, and none of it is a guarantee.

  • Crawl access for search and AI bots: whether robots.txt and server settings let them reach your pages.
  • Index coverage in Google and Bing: which pages are indexed and which aren't, with the reasons where the tools give them.
  • Search impressions: how often your pages appear in search results, from Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
  • A dated prompt log of mentions and links: the questions we asked AI tools, when we asked, and whether your business was mentioned or linked.

AI answers vary from one asking to the next. So we note when and how each check was run, and we don't present a single result as a fixed ranking. Reports describe what we saw and what we changed, in plain language.

Aggarwal et al. (GEO, KDD 2024) found in lab tests that adding citations, quotations and statistics boosted a source's visibility in generative engine responses by up to 40%, and by up to 37% in a Perplexity.ai test. That is a lab benchmark, not a promise, and it is one reason Trendi's guides cite their sources.

What we can't promise

  • We can't promise that any AI tool will mention your business.
  • We can't promise a particular position, rank or number of mentions.
  • We can't promise links. A mention doesn't mean a link.
  • We can't promise results from IndexNow or llms.txt on their own.
  • We can't control how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google or any other engine changes over time.

What we can do is make your site easy to crawl, easy to find for the right searches, and clear enough that an AI tool has good material to work from. Then we check, honestly, what happens.

FAQ

What is an AI visibility audit?

An AI visibility audit is a dated check of whether search and AI bots can reach your pages, whether Google and Bing have indexed them, how often they appear in search, and whether AI tools mention or link to you. It records observations, not guarantees.

Does an AI visibility audit guarantee AI mentions?

No. An audit shows what is happening and what may be getting in the way, such as crawl or index problems. Fixing those can help, but no one can guarantee that an AI tool will mention or link to your business.
