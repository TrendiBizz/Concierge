VOICEMAIL AND CONNECTIONS

Voicemail, and the software you already use

Voicemail is available now. A caller who leaves a message is recorded, added to your CRM as an enquiry, and you are alerted. Concierge can also pass enquiries and bookings on to the tools you already use.

  • ServiceM8: an enquiry can create a job
  • Xero: customers can be sent to Xero as contacts
  • Microsoft 365 calendar: a booked appointment can be added to your calendar
  • Webhooks: signed events sent to your own system when enquiries and bookings happen

Each connection uses your own account and permission, and is tested with you before it is switched on.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Questions before you start

What is Concierge?

Concierge is Trendi’s customer-enquiry and office assistant for website chat, telephone, email and SMS workflows, bookings, lead tracking and social posting with your approval. Each channel is configured and tested before activation. Everything in Concierge is included in the USD $199 a month: full setup and testing, your dedicated business phone number, voice answering, SMS and email confirmations, voicemail, CRM, integrations and social posting.

Where does it get its answers?

From the pages on your website, which we load into its knowledge base. If the answer is not there, it says so and offers to take the visitor's details instead of guessing.

What does Concierge cost?

Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, then it renews monthly. The USD $199 a month Concierge price includes everything in the Concierge offer: full setup and testing, your dedicated business phone number, voice answering, SMS and email confirmations, voicemail, the built-in CRM, integrations and social posting. There are no extras. SEO and AI visibility and penetration testing are separate products, priced separately.

Is it set up for my business?

Yes. We set Concierge up around your business: your own phone number, text and email details, your opening hours, and answers from your own pages. Each channel is tested before it is switched on.

Can it connect to my CRM and email?

Yes. Concierge has its own built-in CRM. If you also use another CRM or email system, we agree and test that integration before activation. Concierge includes a built-in CRM that captures enquiries from your connected calls, emails, texts and website chat, with contact details, conversation history and follow-up status. The Trendi assistant and its core features are the same for every customer.

How will I know what Concierge recorded?

Enquiries from your connected channels are recorded in the built-in CRM. Concierge can answer by email, text or call when needed, using the channels and approval rules you enable. You choose how your team is notified. Voicemail is available now: a recorded message is added to your CRM as an enquiry and you are alerted. Nothing is sent to you or your customers that you did not choose.

Does it promise leads or sales?

No. Concierge answers and records what visitors ask and enter. How many visitors use it, and what they decide, is up to them.

Can it handle phone calls, SMS and email?

Concierge is designed for website chat, phone answering, SMS and email workflows, bookings and follow-up. Trendi provisions and configures your business phone number as part of setup, and each channel is tested with you before activation.

What happens to the conversations?

They are stored so you can review them, along with any contact details a visitor enters. Ask us about retention before you start.

Does it work with Trendi SEO and AI visibility?

They are separate services. SEO and AI visibility helps customers find your site, and Concierge deals with them once they arrive. You can have either one.
