A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Questions before you start

What is Concierge?

Concierge is Trendi’s customer-enquiry and office assistant for website chat, telephone, email and SMS workflows, bookings, lead tracking and approval-controlled social workflows. Each channel is configured and tested before activation.

Where does it get its answers?

From the pages on your website, which we load into its knowledge base. If the answer is not there, it says so and offers to take the visitor's details instead of guessing.

What does Concierge cost?

Concierge is USD $400 a month with a 3-month minimum, then it renews monthly. After the first three months you can move to a yearly plan for USD $4,080, which is 15% off the monthly price. For New Zealand customers, GST is added at checkout and on invoices.

Is it set up for my business?

Yes. We set Concierge up around your business: your own phone number, text and email details, your opening hours, and answers from your own pages. Each channel is tested before it is switched on.

Can it connect to my CRM and email?

Yes. Concierge has its own built-in CRM. If you also use another CRM or email system, we agree and test that integration before activation. Concierge includes a built-in CRM that captures enquiries from your connected calls, emails, texts and website chat, with contact details, conversation history and follow-up status. The Trendi assistant and its core features are the same for every customer.

How will I know what Concierge recorded?

Enquiries from your connected channels are recorded in the built-in CRM. Concierge can answer by email, text or call when needed, using the channels and approval rules you enable. You choose how your team is notified. Voicemail notices are planned. Nothing is sent to you or your customers that you did not choose.

Does it promise leads or sales?

No. Concierge answers and records what visitors ask and enter. How many visitors use it, and what they decide, is up to them.

Can it handle phone calls, SMS and email?

Concierge is designed for website chat, phone answering, SMS and email workflows, bookings and follow-up. Phone, messaging and email features require their respective providers and must be validated before activation for a customer.

What happens to the conversations?

They are stored so you can review them, along with any contact details a visitor enters. Ask us about retention before you start.

Does it work with Trendi SEO and AI visibility?

They are separate services. SEO and AI visibility helps customers find your site, and Concierge deals with them once they arrive. You can have either one.
