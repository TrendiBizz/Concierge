Is Trendi a Profound alternative?

It depends on what you want to buy. If you want a platform that your team operates, that is Profound's category. If you want the work done for you, that is Trendi's.

Both are about being visible in AI answers and search. They reach that goal in different ways. Profound gives a marketing team software to run. Trendi gives a business a service, with fixes, articles and reports handled by us. Read our AI visibility page for how we define the work.

Done-for-you vs an AI marketing platform

What is Profound?

According to Profound's site, checked on 2 November 2026, Profound is "The AI marketing platform". It names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek and Google AI Overviews. It centres on an "AI Marketer" that, in Profound's words, "identifies the work worth doing, drafts it, and pings you for approval".

The products Profound lists are Answer Engine Insights, Prompt Volumes, Shopping, Agent Analytics, AI Marketer, Agents and Context Manager. Its home page says it powers "leading marketing teams". Its solutions pages are written for AEO teams, content teams, PR and brand teams, and agencies. Its plans include AI Marketer credits, and "Get a demo" is the main call to action.

Where does Otterly fit?

Otterly is self-serve AI search monitoring software that you run yourself. Its plans start from USD $29 a month, per otterly.ai/pricing, checked on 2 November 2026. See Trendi vs Otterly for that comparison.

What does each cost?

Profound does not publish a price. Its pricing page, checked on 2 November 2026, shows a trial and an Enterprise plan. The trial is described as being for companies who want to trial Profound before getting a demo. It includes limited AI Marketer credits and 50 prompts run daily for 7 days, and it shows no price. Enterprise is priced "Custom". Profound's pricing FAQ also mentions a self-serve Agency Growth plan with 400 credits per month per client workspace, with no price shown. Please check tryprofound.com/pricing for current details. We do not estimate what Profound costs.

Trendi costs USD $1,500 per month, with a three-month minimum, then month to month. A USD $1,000 deposit secures a start date and is credited against the first invoice. Full details are on our pricing page.

The two models differ. A platform subscription pays for software your team runs. Trendi's price covers the work itself. We do not claim either one is cheaper, because they buy different things.

When does Profound fit?

  • Your marketing team wants to run AI visibility work in-house.
  • You want AI agents and AI search monitoring on one platform.
  • You want to track more answer engines. Profound's Enterprise plan lists up to 9.
  • You need SSO/SAML, SOC 2 compliance and dedicated support, which Profound lists for Enterprise.
  • You would like to try a trial before booking a demo.

When does Trendi fit?

  • You have no in-house team for this work and want it done for you.
  • You want a public price before you talk to anyone.
  • You are anywhere in the world and happy to work online only.
  • You accept that we give no guarantees, and that paid ads, penetration testing, redesign and custom software are not included. Security audits are quoted separately on enquiry.
