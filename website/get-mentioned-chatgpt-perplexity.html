Mention vs link

  • Mention. The answer names your business in the text.
  • Link. The answer includes a URL to your site (or shows you as a source).

A mention is when an AI answer names your business; a link is when it points to your site. They are different outcomes and need separate tracking. Pew Research Center (22 Jul 2025) found that Google users who saw an AI summary clicked a link inside the summary in 1% of visits. In Trendi's observation around Oct 2026, ChatGPT often names brands without linking to them, while Perplexity shows source links more often. That is a snapshot, not a rule, and both products change. For that reason Trendi records mentions and links in separate columns and never counts one as the other. A mention can build awareness without sending a visitor, and a link can send a visitor without the answer recommending you. Neither is guaranteed.

Crawl readiness first

If search and AI search crawlers cannot fetch the page, later steps do not matter. Check robots.txt for Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Claude-SearchBot. Fix blanket Disallow: / mistakes. Keep important text in HTML. Avoid accidental noindex. Submit a sitemap. Use IndexNow for Bing and its partners when you change URLs. Google does not use IndexNow.

Quick checks: AI crawler robots.txt checker · site snapshot. Timing expectations: how long indexing takes.

llms.txt is optional crawl-readiness only. Google Search ignores it for rankings and AI Overviews. It does not buy mentions. See Is llms.txt worth it?.

Pages that answer buyer questions

Pew Research Center (22 Jul 2025) found that 60% of searches starting with question words such as “who”, “what”, “when” or “why” produced an AI summary, against 18% of all Google searches. So Trendi gives each important URL one primary question, with the answer near the top of the page. The page should say who you help, what you sell, where you work and, if you publish prices, what they are. Where buyers compare options, a clear “X vs Y” comparison page answers the question directly. This is ordinary SEO done carefully, not a secret ChatGPT switch. Clear pages help search engines and assistants read your site, but no page is guaranteed to be indexed, found or mentioned.

Corroboration on other sites

Trendi looks for honest third-party signals that back up what your own pages say: directories your business belongs in, listicles you earned a place in, reviews from real customers, and press that is true. Pages that cite their sources also help. Aggarwal et al. (GEO, KDD 2024) found in lab tests that adding citations, quotations and statistics boosted a source's visibility in generative engine responses by up to 40%, and by up to 37% in a Perplexity.ai test. That is a lab benchmark, not a promise. We do not buy or write fake reviews, and we do not place inauthentic mentions. Corroboration does not guarantee a citation.

How to measure

Measurement starts with a fixed prompt set, so each run can be compared with the last. Prompt wording matters, so we keep it unchanged between runs. Trendi runs the set on a schedule and logs each answer as mentioned, linked, partially correct, absent or wrong, with the date and the model used. A set of 20 to 30 prompts is directional, not statistical proof, and answers vary between runs. Our own baseline panel of 25 buyer prompts on 30 Oct 2026 found Trendi mentioned 0 times and linked 0 times in the 13 answers returned so far. We publish it on our sample report, starting point included, so anyone can check it.

What we do not promise

  • No guaranteed ChatGPT or Perplexity mention, citation, or link.
  • No guaranteed Google or Bing rank.
  • No claim that IndexNow or llms.txt alone will get you mentioned.
  • No control over how vendors change retrieval or citation behaviour.

Trendi does not promise mentions, and the reasons are practical. AI vendors change how their products retrieve and cite sources without notice, and the same prompt can return different answers from one run to the next. What can be controlled is narrower: whether your site can be crawled, whether each page is clear about who you help and what you sell, whether the corroboration on other sites is authentic, and whether measurement is honest. Trendi works on those four things. We then report what we observed, with dates, including weeks when nothing changed or the results went the wrong way. If a report says your business was absent from the answers, that is what it will say.

FAQ

Can you rank in ChatGPT search?

There's no guaranteed way to rank in ChatGPT search, and no one can promise a mention or a link. What you can do is make your pages easier to find and cite: allow search bots to crawl them, answer buyer questions clearly, and keep your information accurate and consistent across sources.

How do I get my business mentioned in ChatGPT?

Start with crawlable pages and clear answers to the questions buyers ask. Then make sure accurate details about your business appear on other credible sites too. Track mentions and links separately, with dates. This can help, but no mention is guaranteed.
