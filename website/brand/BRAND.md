# Trendi brand guide

Current brand: **purple "refresh"** (in use on the site since mid-November 2026, made site-wide on 20 Nov 2026).
The navy-and-lime "t" brand is **retired**; its file names below now hold purple artwork so old links still show the current brand.

On 20 Nov 2026 the wordmark was converted from typed Arial text to drawn outlines (Arial Bold glyphs), so it looks identical on every device and needs no font. Never retype it in a font.

### Rules
- Every page uses one icon set: `<link rel="icon" href="/brand/favicon.svg">`, `<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/brand/apple-touch-icon.png">`, `<meta name="theme-color" content="#7142d6">`, absolute paths only.
- Organization schema `logo` is `https://trendibizz.com/brand/icon-512.png`.
- Brand assets are cached for 30 days at Cloudflare; when artwork changes, bump the `?v=` on every reference.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to a quarter of the tile height. Minimum size: tile 16 px, horizontal logo 90 px wide.
- Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows. Don't put the logo on busy photos without a solid white or light-purple panel behind it.
- **Concierge icons (Jo, 2026-11-23): the purple tile with the white service bell is the only Concierge logo.** It is the favicon (`/favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`), the apple-touch-icon, and the Studio install icons (`web/portal/public/*`), all generated by `scripts/brand/make_pwa_icons.py` from `scripts/brand/concierge-icon/`. The Trendi wordmark and `mark-refresh.svg` still mean Trendi the company. The product PNGs under `website/brand/products/` were restyled to the purple palette on 20 Nov 2026 (`scripts/build_chatgpt_product_feed.py`).

## Type

- **Wordmark:** lowercase, drawn shapes. Never retype it in a font.
- **Website:** the system font stack (fast, no downloads). If you later want a custom font, a geometric sans such as Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans (self-hosted) suits the rounded logo.
- Headlines: bold, tight tracking. Body: regular, generous line height.

## Voice

Plain, short and confident. Say what the work is, not what it might do. No guarantees we can't keep. Lowercase "trendi" only in the logo; write "Trendi" in sentences.

## Before launch

Search IPONZ (NZ trade marks) and the web for existing "Trendi" marks in marketing, SEO and software before investing further in the brand.
