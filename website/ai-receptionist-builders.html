Built around project enquiries

Concierge answers questions about your services, coverage and process from your own website, captures the customer’s name, contact details and project notes, and records the enquiry for follow-up. It can offer agreed site-visit times.

For cladding, re-cladding and building work, it can ask what the project involves and whether plans are available, then pass the details to you.

Why trades lose enquiries

Customers often enquire in the evening or while you are on a roof. A missed call or a slow reply can lose the job. Concierge answers the routine part, records the enquiry and gives you the context for a proper conversation.

It does not price a project or promise a quote. It captures the details so you can.

Set up around your business

We load your website into its knowledge, configure the channels you want and test each one. Phone and SMS require providers; website chat works without a number. Nothing is sent to a customer that you have not approved.

Questions

Can an AI receptionist handle builder enquiries?

It answers routine questions, captures project details and books agreed site visits. Complex or commercial conversations are passed to you with the context.

Can it take plan drawings?

Your team can receive plans through the Concierge design upload link, and an IFC model can provide wall areas for cladding quotes. PDF and DWG plans are reviewed by a person.

Does it price the job?

No. It captures the enquiry and details. Pricing and the quotation are reviewed and signed off by the business owner.

Does it work after hours?

Yes, for the hours you enable. Phone and SMS channels require providers and testing.

How much does it cost?

USD $250 a month with a 3-month minimum, then monthly. Provider charges for phone and SMS are separate.
