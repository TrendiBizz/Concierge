What an AI answering service does

A traditional answering service is a call centre that takes a message. An AI answering service answers the call itself, asks the questions you set, answers routine questions from your own information, and records the enquiry in a CRM with a transcript.

Concierge covers the phone, website chat, email and SMS from one place, so a customer who rings, then texts, then emails is one conversation, not three.

When it answers, and when a person should

You choose the hours and the rules: after hours only, when you are busy, or always. Anything the assistant should not decide, such as a complaint, a quote outside your price list or a safety issue, is flagged for you to follow up rather than guessed at.

What we set up with you

A business phone number, your opening hours, your services and prices, the questions to ask on every call, your calendar, and who is told when something needs a person. Each channel is tested before it goes live. If a channel cannot be tested in your country, we tell you before you pay.

What happens after a call is answered

Every call becomes an enquiry in your Concierge portal with the caller number, time, a summary and the transcript. Contacts and conversations are kept together in the built-in CRM. You are alerted by email for each enquiry, and optionally in Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Control stays with you

You can take over any individual conversation from the portal, and you can pause Concierge for your whole business with one action; messages are still saved while it is paused. The assistant only answers from the information and rules you approved, and anything outside them is flagged rather than guessed at.

How it compares with a message-taking service

A traditional answering service reads from a script and passes you a message. Concierge also books into your calendar where your rules allow, keeps the transcript, and records the enquiry in the CRM that comes with it, so follow-up does not depend on someone retyping a message. The trade-off is that it is an AI assistant, which is why the greeting says so and why you decide what it may and may not handle.

Questions

Is an AI answering service the same as a virtual receptionist?

They overlap. A virtual receptionist is usually a person working remotely. Concierge is an AI assistant that answers for you and passes on what needs a human. See the virtual receptionist page for the comparison.

How much does it cost?

Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum. Phone and SMS provider charges are separate.

Does it answer calls in my business name?

Yes, once a number and voice provider are connected and tested. The greeting, tone and what it may say are agreed with you.

What if a caller needs a person?

Anything outside the rules you set is recorded and flagged for you to follow up.

Does it guarantee more customers?

No. It reduces missed enquiries and records them properly. We do not promise leads, sales or rankings.
