What happens after hours

When a customer calls or messages outside your opening hours, Concierge answers from your business information, takes their name and contact details, offers an agreed appointment time where appropriate, and records the conversation in your CRM.

You choose the hours it covers and how your team is notified. If a question needs a person, it says so and captures the details for a callback.

Why after-hours enquiries matter

Many customers search and enquire when their own day ends. A missed call at 7pm can become a booking for a competitor by 9am. Answering and recording the enquiry keeps the opportunity with your business.

We do not promise leads or sales. Concierge helps make sure the enquiry is answered and recorded.

Set up and testing

Phone, email and SMS channels are configured with your providers and tested before activation. Website chat works without a phone number. Availability depends on the providers in your country.

Questions

What is an after-hours answering service?

It answers customer calls and messages outside normal business hours, captures the enquiry and passes it to the team. Concierge is an AI assistant that does this from your own business information.

How much does it cost?

Concierge is USD $400 a month with a 3-month minimum. Phone and SMS provider charges are separate. Website chat is included.

Can it book appointments after hours?

Yes, for agreed appointment times and hours. You choose the availability and appointment length; the booking is kept with the enquiry.

Will it answer every call?

It answers the calls and messages you enable. A phone number and voice provider must be connected and tested. Complex conversations are passed to a person with the context.

Does it promise more bookings?

No. It helps make sure after-hours enquiries are answered and recorded. Demand and conversion are outside our control.
