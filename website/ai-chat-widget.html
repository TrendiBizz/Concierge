What the widget does

The chat window answers customer questions from your website content, captures a name plus a phone number or email with permission, offers agreed appointment times and records the conversation. It can hand over to your team when needed.

It is not a generic chatbot: it is configured around your business and tested with the questions your customers ask.

One line to install

Add the script to your site, or send it to your web developer. The widget is served from Trendi’s own domain and the chat API is origin-locked to your site. No plugin or theme changes are required.

worldwide

Concierge works in your timezone and opening hours. Phone and SMS channels require local providers; website chat works without a phone number. Each channel is tested before activation.

Questions

How do I add the chat widget?

Add the script before the closing body tag on your site. We provide the exact snippet and can test it with you.

Does it answer from my website?

Yes. We load the public pages you own into its knowledge and it answers from that information. If the answer is not there, it offers to pass the question on.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, for agreed hours and rules, once booking is enabled by the owner.

How much does it cost?

USD $250 a month with a 3-month minimum. Phone and SMS provider charges are separate.

Is the chat private?

Each business has a private CRM workspace. The chat API is origin-locked to your site and conversations are stored for your business.
