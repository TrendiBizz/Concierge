An Allo alternative for any business

Allo is a business phone system priced per seat, with an AI receptionist as an add-on. Concierge is an AI office assistant at one monthly price. This page says where each fits.

The short version

Choose Allo if you want a new business phone and SMS system for your team, with an AI receptionist on top. Choose Concierge if you want an AI assistant answering calls, chat, email and SMS for you, set up and tested, without changing your team's phones.

Price, as published

From Allo's own pricing page (checked 25 November 2026): the Business plan is $32 a seat a month billed yearly or $45 month to month, and the AI Receptionist is an add-on at $32 a month per agent. It lists a 3-day free trial, a 30-day money-back period, no setup fees, no contracts, and says pricing is early and can still change. See withallo.com/pricing for current figures.

Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, including setup, testing, a business phone number, voice answering, SMS and email confirmations, voicemail, CRM and integrations. Provider charges for calls and SMS are separate.

Where Allo fits better

Allo is a phone system as well as an answering assistant: numbers in 80+ countries, free number porting, unlimited national calls, inbound SMS, and its AI receptionist can transfer to a person. It has no contract and a free trial. It does not publish call limits for the AI receptionist on the pages we read, so check them before comparing price.

What Concierge adds

A flat price for the assistant itself, website chat and email as well as phone and SMS, a built-in CRM, and setup and testing done with you.

Questions

Is Concierge an Allo replacement?

Not as a phone system. Concierge does not replace your team's phones; it answers enquiries and records them. For the AI receptionist part, it is an alternative.

How much does Allo cost compared with Concierge?

Allo's Business plan is $32 a seat a month billed yearly ($45 monthly) plus $32 a month for the AI Receptionist (checked 25 November 2026). Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum.

Which is cheaper?

For one seat with the AI receptionist, Allo's published prices are lower. Concierge covers more channels and includes setup. Compare the features you need.
