Who the partner programme is for

Web developers and agencies

You build and launch sites on WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Framer, Squarespace, Wix, Bubble or custom code. After launch, a site still needs to be readable by search engines and AI crawlers, with pages fixed and new content written.

Trendi does that after-launch work. We do not do redesigns or custom software, so we do not compete with you. Your client keeps the site you built, and you have a clear answer when they ask what comes next.

Accountants and bookkeepers

Clients ask which marketing help is worth paying for. If your practice uses Xero, QuickBooks, MYOB or Sage, you can point them to a defined service with a stated price and a short weekly report.

You do not need to do any marketing yourself. You refer, and we do the work.

What your clients get

Trendi costs USD $1,500 per month, with a three-month minimum and month to month after that. A USD $1,000 deposit secures a start date and is credited against the first invoice.

  • Index checks so search engines and AI crawlers can read the site
  • Page fixes: titles, copy, speed and internal links
  • Keyword research and a search map
  • In-depth articles each month, plus comparison and alternatives pages
  • AI mention tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, with mentions and links logged separately
  • Fact checks, and a short report every week

We do not promise rankings, traffic or AI mentions to anyone.

What you get

Your reward is 10% of the referred client's first payment. The first payment is the client's first USD $1,500 monthly invoice, with any deposit credited against it, so the reward is USD $150 per referred client.

It is paid 30 days after the client's payment clears, by Wise, bank transfer or PayPal, whichever you choose. Nothing is paid if the client's payment is refunded.

Tax may apply to referral payments, depending on your own tax situation. We make no promise about how many clients you refer or how many of them start.

How it works

  1. Ask for your code

    Email admin@trendibizz.com with the subject "Partner code request" and your business name and website. We reply with a short referral code, for example DEV-ACME. Joining is opt-in only.

  2. Share your link

    Your link looks like trendibizz.com/contact?ref=DEV-ACME. The contact form has an optional Referral code field that fills in from the link, and a client can also type the code. Tell the client you receive a referral payment.

  3. We confirm and pay

    We ask for and confirm the code with the client in our scoping reply, before the client pays anything. We also mention the referral to the client at that point. We pay your reward 30 days after the client's payment clears, by Wise, bank transfer or PayPal, whichever you choose.

Fair-play rules

  • Businesses only: partners and the clients they refer are businesses.
  • No self-referrals.
  • No reward for clients who are already working with us or already in talks with us.
  • One partner per client. The first valid code wins.
  • Disclosure both ways: you tell the people you refer that you receive a referral payment, and we mention the referral to the client when we confirm the code.
  • Refunds: nothing is paid if the client's payment is refunded.
  • Do not promise rankings, traffic or AI mentions on Trendi's behalf, and do not send unsolicited bulk email or spam about Trendi.
  • Tax on referral payments depends on your own tax situation. We send these terms to you in writing with your code.

FAQ

Do you redesign websites?

No. We do not do website redesigns or custom software. We work on a site after it is built: index checks, page fixes, articles and AI visibility work. That is why we do not compete with the developers and agencies who refer clients to us.

Do you work with clients outside my country?

Yes. We work online with businesses worldwide, so a client's location does not affect a referral. Our price is USD $1,500 per month.

When am I paid?

You are paid 30 days after the client's payment clears, by Wise, bank transfer or PayPal, whichever you choose. We confirm the code with the client before they pay, so it is clear who referred them. Nothing is paid if the client's payment is refunded.

Do I need to sell or do any marketing work?

No. You share your link or code and tell the client you receive a referral payment. We answer the enquiry, scope the work, do the work and send the client a short weekly report. You do not need to explain SEO or AI visibility.

Will my client know I referred them?

Yes. You tell them you receive a referral payment when you refer them, and we mention the referral when we confirm your code in our scoping reply. This happens before the client pays anything, so nothing is hidden from them.
