What to include in your message

One sentence is enough to start, and a few details help us scope the work.

  • Your website address, so we know which pages to look at.
  • One question your customers ask, in the words they use.
  • The markets or countries you want to be found in, if that matters to you.
  • Anything you already know is wrong, such as pages that do not appear in search or sources that describe you inaccurately.
  • Whether you want to start monthly or with a deposit that secures a start date. See pricing for how each works.
  • A referral code, if someone sent you. The form has an optional field for it.

We read your message and reply within one business day. Business days are on NZ time; see when we reply. Our reply covers whether Trendi is a fit, what access we would need and a clear scope.

The scope is agreed before any work starts. You can start monthly, or pay a deposit that secures a start date.

Once work begins, we check changes and send a short weekly report. The aim is a site that is Indexed, Found and Mentioned, and we track mentions and links separately. We do not guarantee rankings or AI mentions.

When we reply (NZ time)

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding NZ public holidays, on Pacific/Auckland time (UTC+13 in summer, UTC+12 in winter). Replies may arrive overnight in your time zone.

Before you get in touch

You can look at your own site first. These are optional and you do not need them to contact us.

  • Run the Site snapshot for public crawl readiness and homepage signals.
  • Use the AI crawler robots.txt checker to see how your robots.txt treats AI crawlers.
  • Try the Website security check for an informational HTTPS and headers scan. It is not a penetration test. None of these tools check rankings or AI mentions.
  • Read the sample report to see the weekly format: Scorecard, Diagnosis, Fix list and Trend. It uses our own site's dated baseline and is not a client.
  • Read about our service, the questions people ask before they start and pricing and payment questions.
