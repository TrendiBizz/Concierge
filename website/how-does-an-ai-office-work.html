A PLAIN GUIDE

How does an AI receptionist work? An AI office guide

An AI receptionist, or AI office, answers on the channels you connect and replies only from the pages and business information you approve. With the person's permission it takes their details, it offers the appointment times you have agreed, and it passes complex or sensitive questions to a person with the full context.

ONE CONVERSATION

What happens during one conversation

Here is the path a single enquiry takes, from the first message to the follow-up.

  1. It greets the person and says it is an AI

    Every conversation carries a plain notice that the visitor is chatting with an AI, that it can make mistakes, and that conversations may be saved so the team can follow up.

  2. It works out what they are asking

    A visitor might ask about opening hours, a service or how to book. The assistant reads the question and looks for the answer.

  3. It answers from approved content, or says it does not know

    If the answer is in the pages and business information you approved, it replies. If not, it says so and offers to pass the question on.

  4. It asks for details, with permission

    It collects a name plus a phone number or email, with the person's permission to be contacted.

  5. It offers a booking

    If you want bookings, it offers the appointment times you have agreed and books one, keeping the booking with the enquiry.

  6. It saves the record

    The enquiry goes into the built-in CRM with the conversation history. If you already use a CRM, we agree how to connect it before you start and test it first.

  7. Your team is prompted to follow up

    When an enquiry is waiting, your team is prompted to follow up, and the conversation history stays together.

WHERE ANSWERS COME FROM

Where the answers come from

An AI office is only as good as what it is allowed to say. Concierge answers from two things: your approved public website pages and business information, and a short set of written rules.

The rules say what it answers, what goes to a person, and who to reach when something is urgent. We write them down with you during setup, then try the questions your customers ask before anything goes live.

It is set up not to invent answers. When the information is not there, it says so and offers to pass the question on, instead of guessing. You can read every conversation, so a gap shows up and can be fixed.

For the full onboarding process, see how setup works, step by step. This guide is about the conversation, not the setup.

CHANNELS

Can AI answer phone calls?

Yes, on your business phone number. Trendi sets the number up for you in minutes, and the channel is tested before it goes live. It can then answer, take a message and record the enquiry.

The other channels work like this, in one line each:

  • Website chat: one line of code adds the chat window, which answers from your pages, captures details and offers booking times.
  • Email: customer email becomes a reviewable draft in your CRM, and replies are approved before anything is sent.
  • SMS: text enquiries and follow-ups through a number you connect. Delivery depends on your paired smartphone and mobile network.
  • Social: Concierge helps you prepare and approve posts. It is not an answering channel.

Phone, email and SMS are enabled only after provider setup and testing. If something cannot be tested in your country, we say so rather than switching it on. For the missed-call side, see missed-call text back, and for cover outside office hours, after-hours answering.

WHAT VISITORS SEE

What does an AI office sound like?

Rather than describe a voice, here is what a visitor sees on trendibizz.com. The chat window opens from a button in the corner of the page. It shows who the visitor is talking to, a greeting written for the business and a box to type a question.

Two lines stay under the box in every conversation. One is an optional checkbox to record the conversation for the business, with audio kept for 30 days. The other is a plain notice that the visitor is chatting with an AI, that it can make mistakes, and that conversations may be saved.

The window also has a microphone button for a voice conversation in the browser. It only starts after the visitor allows microphone access, and if voice drops the visitor can keep typing.

The notice is there on purpose. Visitors should know they are not talking to a person, and you should be able to read what was said.

HANDOVER

When a person takes over

Routine questions are the assistant's job. Complex or sensitive conversations go to a person, with the full context.

The handover rules are agreed in setup: which enquiries a person must handle, and the order of people to reach when something is urgent. You review the greeting, the handover rules and a set of real test conversations before it goes live.

How does this compare with a person answering for you? Concierge can answer every hour you enable, including outside office hours, for a fixed monthly subscription rather than a per-call charge. People are better at negotiation, nuance and difficult calls. Our side-by-side is in AI answering service or human answering service, and the virtual receptionist page covers that angle too.

HONEST LIMITS

What it does not do

  • It answers in English.
  • It can make mistakes, which is why the notice sits under every conversation and you can read every conversation.
  • Phone, SMS and email work only once a provider is connected and tested.
  • It does not take payments. Payment handoff or checkout has to be agreed and verified first.
  • Voicemail is available now: a caller who leaves one is recorded, added to your CRM as an enquiry and you are alerted. It is not a replacement for a person on urgent matters.
  • It is the wrong buy if most of your enquiries are complex, bespoke or high value. A human answering service is likely the better fit, and we will tell you so.
  • It does not promise leads, sales, bookings or rankings.

The same list is on the what it does not do section of our AI office page.
