SECURITY & CONFIDENCE

Vulnerability disclosure

Direct answer: If you find a security issue on trendibizz.com or related Trendi systems we operate, email admin@trendibizz.com.

We prefer coordinated disclosure: tell us first, give us a reasonable window to investigate and fix, then publish if you choose.

How to report

  • Email admin@trendibizz.com with a clear description, affected URL or component, and steps to reproduce where safe.
  • Include your preferred contact method if it is not the From address.
  • Do not include passwords, personal data of third parties, or exploit chains that would harm customers.

What we ask

  • Act in good faith. Do not access data that is not yours, and do not degrade the service.
  • Avoid social engineering of staff or customers, physical attacks, and denial-of-service testing unless we agreed that scope in writing first.
  • Give us a reasonable time to respond before public disclosure (we aim to acknowledge within a few business days).

What this page is not

  • Not a bug bounty with fixed payouts (we may thank researchers; payment is not guaranteed).
  • Not permission to test third-party sites, customer sites, or systems you do not own.
  • Not the same as our website security check tool or paid penetration testing service.

Our security.txt

Machine-readable contact details live at /.well-known/security.txt. The Policy field points to this page.
