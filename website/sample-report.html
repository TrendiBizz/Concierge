What is in every weekly report

1. Scorecard

One line each for Indexed, Found and Mentioned, with every figure dated and sourced.

You see where you stand on each measure and can check where the number came from.

2. Diagnosis

A few plain-English sentences on what the scorecard means and where the gap is.

You know which measure needs the work, without reading raw data.

3. Fix list

Dated changes, each marked shipped, underway or next.

You can see exactly what was done, what is in progress and what comes after.

4. Trend

A week-by-week table with one row per week, starting from a Week 0 baseline.

You can see progress, or the lack of it, against the same line every week.

The scorecard: Indexed, Found, Mentioned

Read the three lines from top to bottom: Indexed is whether search engines can reach and store your pages, Found is whether customers' searches surface them, and Mentioned is whether AI answers name you. Mentions and links are reported in separate columns and never counted as each other.

Diagnosis, in plain English

Indexed: done. All 17 of 17 sitemap URLs are "Submitted and indexed", and Google's canonical matches ours on all 17 (Google Search Console URL Inspection, 30 Oct 2026). Search engines can reach, read and store every page we list.

Found: early. Google shows 19 impressions, 0 clicks and an average position of 2.79 for 4 to 31 Oct 2026. The only query Google shows is "trendi", our own name, with 2 impressions; the rest came without a query Google would show us. Bing shows 0 impressions and 0 clicks (31 Oct 2026). Being found for buyer searches is the first gap to close.

Mentioned: zero so far. Across 25 buyer prompts run on 30 Oct 2026, Gemini (no web search) answered 13 of 25 with 0 mentions and 0 links, and Trendi was in 0 of 25 Google NZ top 10s. Established sites fill those results: reddit.com (11), ahrefs.com (5) and semrush.com (5). That gap is what the work targets. Nobody can promise it closes by a set date.

What the baseline does not cover yet

  • No ChatGPT or Perplexity run yet. The 30 Oct 2026 panel used Gemini only, and 12 of the 25 prompts have no answer yet.
  • No real-user speed data. PageSpeed Insights returned no Chrome UX Report field data for the site on 31 Oct 2026.
  • No Bing query data. Bing Webmaster Tools returned no queries for the 3 days available.
  • No search volumes for our target terms. We have not pulled keyword volumes, so none are shown.
  • Bing crawl statistics are left out. The 28 Oct 2026 crawl report counted 16 client-error responses and 497 URLs “in index”, against 17 sitemap URLs. We have not yet confirmed what those counts cover, so we are not reporting them until we have.

Caveats

  • The two speed readings come from different tools on different days (Lighthouse CLI on 30 Oct 2026, PageSpeed Insights on 31 Oct 2026), and both are lab tests, not real-user data. The change between them is not a before/after claim.
  • The panel is a proxy, not proof of what any person sees in ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google. Gemini answered without web search, and only 13 of the 25 prompts have been answered so far.
  • 0 clicks and 0 mentions is our real starting point. Saying so plainly is the point of this page.

FAQ

Is this a real client report?

No. It is our own site, trendibizz.com, shown at a Day-0 baseline so you can see the format on numbers you can check. We do not present it as a client result.

Why are the baseline numbers so low?

The domain was relaunched in October 2026, and a relaunched domain starts near zero. Week 0 is the line later weeks are measured against. Indexed is already complete, and Found and Mentioned are where the work goes.

How often does a client get a report?

Weekly. Each report has the same four parts: Scorecard, Diagnosis, Fix list and Trend, with a new row added to the Trend table.

Does Trendi promise rankings or AI mentions?

No. We tell you what we do and what we measure. Rankings, clicks, citations and AI mentions depend on things outside our control, so we do not promise them.
