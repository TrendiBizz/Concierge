A Goodcall alternative for small business

Goodcall is an AI phone agent priced by the number of unique customers who call. Concierge covers phone, chat, email and SMS for one price. This page says where each fits.

The short version

Choose Goodcall if you mainly want a phone agent, your call volume is low and predictable, and you want to start with a free demo agent. Choose Concierge if you want calls, website chat, email and SMS handled in one place, with the setup done and tested for you.

Price, as published

From Goodcall's own pricing page (checked 25 November 2026): Starter $79 a month for 100 unique customers, Growth $129 for 500 and Scale $299 for 2,000, with extra customers at 79¢, 26¢ and 15¢ each. It states there are no per-minute charges, and every plan includes a local number. Enterprise is quote-only. See goodcall.com/pricing for current figures.

Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, including setup, testing, a business phone number, voice answering, SMS and email confirmations, voicemail, CRM and integrations. Provider charges for calls and SMS are separate.

Where Goodcall fits better

At low volume Goodcall's Starter plan costs less than Concierge. Goodcall also publishes call transfer to a list of people, a free demo agent you can try before connecting a number, and SOC 2 and HIPAA compliance statements. Its pages show English only. We have not verified its contract or cancellation terms because they were not on the pages we read.

What Concierge adds

Chat, email and SMS alongside the phone, one conversation history per customer, a built-in CRM, and configuration and testing done with you before activation.

Questions

Is Concierge a Goodcall replacement?

For answering calls, capturing enquiries and booking, yes. Goodcall is phone-focused; Concierge also handles chat, email and SMS.

How much does Goodcall cost compared with Concierge?

Goodcall publishes $79, $129 and $299 a month plans by unique customers per month (checked 25 November 2026). Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, with provider call and SMS charges separate.

Which is cheaper?

It depends on volume. At around 100 unique customers a month Goodcall's Starter plan is cheaper. Work out your own number of callers before choosing.
