AI receptionist vs call center outsourcing

Call center outsourcing means a company supplies agents who answer for your business. A human BPO (business process outsourcer) staffs that with people, and the model suits large or complex support work. Concierge is an AI phone agent that answers in your business name, follows the rules you approve and records each enquiry in a built-in CRM with a transcript.

The two are not the same product. Concierge answers enquiries for a service business and books appointments. It does not look up orders in an online store or work tickets in a help desk, so a Shopify store that needs order-and-returns support is better served by a support-focused outsourced call center.

Who each option suits

Choose a human BPO if you need people on large volumes, calls that turn on judgement, or support work inside tools such as a help desk. Choose Concierge if the main job is picking up enquiries for a small or medium service business: who called, what they need, can they book, who should follow up.

Many businesses use both. The AI takes the first call and routine questions, and your own in-house team handles the complex or on-site work. That hybrid is covered in AI answering service vs human.

Call center outsourcing cost and pricing transparency

Ringly's guide to call center outsourcing companies describes three common pricing models: per-agent, per-minute and per-ticket, with setup fees and minimum monthly commitments to watch for (ringly.io, checked 27 November 2026). Each bills by usage, so the monthly total moves with your volume.

Concierge is one price: USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Setup and testing are included. Provider charges for calls and SMS are separate. We have not compared that with any particular provider's rates, so ask each one for a written quote and work out your own call numbers.
