How the figure is worked out

Three multiplications, nothing hidden:

  • Missed calls per week × average job value × close rate = revenue you never quoted for, per week.
  • × 4.33 = a month (52 weeks ÷ 12, not "four weeks").
  • × 52 = a year.

The close rate is the honest part. Not every enquiry becomes work, and a missed call is not automatically a lost job, some callers ring back, some go elsewhere quietly. Using your own close rate keeps the estimate tied to reality instead of flattering it. If you do not know it, divide the quotes you won last month by the enquiries you received, and use that.

We deliberately do not add "lifetime customer value", referrals, or the review you never got. Those are real, but they are guesses, and a guess multiplied twelve times stops being an estimate and starts being a sales tactic.

What this deliberately leaves out

  • Callers who ring back. Some do. A callback does not restore the first impression, and it rarely happens when the caller had three other numbers to try.
  • After-hours calls. The calculator covers a week, so if you close at 5pm, the calls between 5pm and 9am are inside the number if you counted them.
  • Reputation and referrals. Unanswered calls are often invisible in reviews, people do not leave a review for a business that never picked up. That cost is real but unmeasured, so it stays out of the sum.
  • Tax and costs. This is revenue, not profit. Costs of doing the work, materials, subcontractors, your time, are not deducted. Gross revenue lost is the right first number; margin is a second conversation about your own business.

What actually reduces the number

There are only four levers, in order of cost:

  1. Answer more of the calls you already get. A missed call is usually a timing problem, not a demand problem. Voicemail is not an answer; most callers hang up before the beep.
  2. Cover the hours you are closed. After-hours answering catches the calls that arrive when nobody is there, which for trades is often the best enquiries of the day.
  3. Text back what you missed. A missed-call text back keeps the enquiry alive long enough for you to ring from site.
  4. Put something on the phone that answers in your voice. An AI receptionist takes the routine questions, captures the job details and books agreed times, and passes anything sensitive to a person. It does not replace judgement on price or scope.

Questions about how this calculator works

Does the calculator send my numbers anywhere?

No. The arithmetic runs in your browser with a small script served from this site. The share link puts your three numbers in the address bar so you can bookmark or send it; nothing is transmitted to Trendi unless you contact us.

What counts as a missed call?

Any inbound call where the caller did not reach a person: it rang out, hit voicemail, was abandoned in a queue, or arrived while the line was busy. Calls you chose to decline after speaking, spam, suppliers, should not be counted.
