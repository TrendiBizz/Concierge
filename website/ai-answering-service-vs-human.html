AI VS HUMAN

AI answering service vs human answering service

Both answer your customers. They are good at different things. Concierge handles routine questions, capture and booking around the clock; people remain better at negotiation, empathy and difficult calls. The practical answer is usually a mix, with one CRM holding the whole conversation.

SIDE BY SIDE

Where each one fits

Availability

AI can answer every hour you enable, including nights and weekends, without rosters. A human service needs staff or on-call cover.

Cost model

Concierge is a fixed monthly subscription. Human answering services usually charge per call, per minute or per agent hour.

Complex calls

People are better at negotiation, complaints, nuance and high-value commercial conversations. Concierge should hand those over with context.

Consistency

AI follows the same rules every time and answers from the information you approve. People bring judgement and warmth, but can vary.

Data

Concierge records every enquiry in your own CRM. A human service may record messages in its own system and hand them over later.

Scaling

AI absorbs sudden spikes without new staff. A human service may need extra capacity for busy periods, with extra cost.

THE PRACTICAL ANSWER

Use both, with clear rules

Most businesses do not choose one forever. They let Concierge answer routine enquiries, capture details and book, and route anything sensitive or commercial to a person. You decide the rules, review the conversations and keep everything in one CRM.

  • Routine questions, hours, availability and booking: AI.
  • Complaints, negotiation, legal or high-value calls: a person.
  • Out of hours: AI captures the enquiry, a person follows up.
  • Every channel recorded in one place for review.

QUESTIONS

AI vs human questions

What is the difference between an AI answering service and a human answering service?

An AI answering service uses software to answer routine enquiries from chat, email, SMS or phone. A human answering service uses people. Concierge answers from your own business information, captures details and books appointments; people remain better for negotiation and difficult conversations.

Is an AI receptionist cheaper than a human answering service?

Usually the subscription is more predictable than per-call pricing, but the total depends on volume, the channels you enable, provider charges and the human time you keep. Trendi Concierge is USD $400 a month with a 3-month minimum; provider charges are separate.

Can an AI receptionist handle every call?

No. It handles routine questions, capture and booking. Complex, emotional or high-value conversations should go to a person, and Concierge is designed to hand over with the context.

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?

Concierge is instructed to be clear that it is an AI assistant. We do not recommend pretending otherwise. You can review the greeting and the rules it follows.

Can I use both an AI and a human service?

Yes. Many businesses use Concierge for routine enquiries and out-of-hours cover, and keep people for complex or commercial conversations. The CRM keeps both in one place.

Does an AI answering service work worldwide?

Yes, for English-language conversations. The channels that are available depend on the providers operating in your country, and each channel is tested before activation.
