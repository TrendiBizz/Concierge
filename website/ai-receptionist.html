WHAT AN AI RECEPTIONIST DOES

The front desk work, answered and recorded

A receptionist answers, qualifies and routes enquiries. Concierge does the routine part from the channels you connect, then keeps every detail in one place for your team.

Answer

Replies to customers using your own website pages and business information. If the answer is not there, it says so and offers to pass the question on.

Capture

Collects a name plus a phone number or email, with the customer’s permission to be contacted, and creates an enquiry in the built-in CRM.

Book

Offers agreed appointment times and books them, keeping the booking and its details with the enquiry.

Follow up

Tracks what happens next, prompts your team when an enquiry is waiting and keeps the conversation history together.

Route the right way

Handles routine questions and passes complex or sensitive conversations to a person, with the full context.

Stay in your systems

Uses your own contact details and, where agreed and tested, connects to your existing CRM or email. If you have no CRM, Concierge keeps one for you.

AI VS HUMAN

AI receptionist compared with a human answering service

Availability

Concierge can answer every hour you enable it, including outside office hours. A human service needs staffing or on-call arrangements.

Cost

Concierge is a fixed monthly subscription, not a per-call charge. Human answering services usually price per call or per minute.

Complex conversations

People are better at negotiation, nuance and difficult calls. Concierge is designed for routine answering, capture and booking, and should hand over when a person is needed.

Concierge is an assistant, not a replacement for every human role. We do not promise a reduction in headcount or costs.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

AI receptionist questions

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist answers routine customer enquiries from channels such as website chat, email, SMS and phone. Concierge answers from your own business information, captures contact details, offers agreed appointment times and records everything in a CRM for your team.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

Trendi Concierge is USD $400 a month with a 3-month minimum, then it renews monthly. After the first three months you can move to a yearly plan at USD $4,080. Prices are in USD; We do not promise leads or sales.

Does it answer phone calls?

It can answer a connected business number, take a message and record the enquiry. A phone number and voice provider are required, and each channel is tested before activation. Telephone availability depends on the provider and the country.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, for agreed appointment times and hours. Concierge offers times, books the appointment and keeps the booking with the enquiry. You choose the hours, appointment length and rules.

Does it replace reception staff?

No. It handles routine answering, capture and booking, and hands complex or sensitive conversations to a person with the context. It is an assistant, not a replacement for every human role.

Does it work outside my country?

Yes. Trendi works with businesses worldwide. The assistant answers in English, uses your business hours and timezone, and the channels available depend on the providers that operate in your country.

Is my business data private?

Each business has its own private CRM workspace. Enquiries, contacts and conversations are kept for that business. Access is controlled by owner, editor and viewer roles. Ask us about data retention and any integration before you start.

Does an AI receptionist guarantee more leads?

No. It helps make sure enquiries are answered and recorded rather than missed. How many customers arrive and what they decide is outside our control, and we do not promise leads, sales or rankings.
