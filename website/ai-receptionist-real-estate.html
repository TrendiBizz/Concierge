AI receptionist for real estate

Property enquiries do not wait for office hours. Concierge answers buyer and tenant questions from your listings and website, captures the contact and books agreed viewings — then records everything in your CRM.

For sales and rentals

Concierge can answer questions about a listing, your process and coverage, capture the enquirer’s name, phone and email, and offer agreed viewing times. It records the enquiry against the property or the general sales pipeline.

Complex negotiations and offers go to the agent with the context. Concierge does not value a property or promise a sale.

After-hours and busy periods

Enquiries often arrive in the evening or during viewings. Concierge answers the routine part, captures the details and books agreed times, so the agent starts the next day with a list rather than voicemail.

Privacy and consent

Contact details are captured with permission to be contacted. Call recording, where enabled, asks the caller first and discards audio if consent is withdrawn. Each business controls its own CRM and retention.

Questions

Can an AI receptionist book property viewings?

Yes, for agreed times and rules. It offers viewing times, books the appointment and keeps it with the enquiry. You choose availability and appointment length.

Does it know my listings?

It answers from the public pages and listing information you load into its knowledge. If the answer is not there, it offers to pass the question to the team.

Does it replace an agent?

No. It handles routine enquiries, capture and booking, and passes negotiations and complex questions to a person.

How much does it cost?

USD $250 a month with a 3-month minimum. Phone and SMS provider charges are separate.

Does it guarantee more listings?

No. It helps make sure enquiries are answered and recorded. We do not promise leads, listings or sales.
