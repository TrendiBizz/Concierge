Questions

Can an AI receptionist give medical advice?

No. Concierge is instructed not to diagnose or give clinical advice. It handles routine reception questions and passes clinical or urgent matters to a person.

Is patient data private?

Each practice has a private workspace with role-controlled access. Contact details and conversations are kept for that practice. Data retention and integrations are agreed before activation.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, for agreed times and rules. It offers times, books the appointment and keeps it with the enquiry.

Does it replace reception staff?

No. It handles routine answering, capture and booking and hands complex or sensitive conversations to your team.

How much does it cost?

USD $250 a month with a 3-month minimum. Provider charges for phone and SMS are separate.

Can it answer questions about test results or medication?

No. It is instructed not to discuss results, medication or symptoms. Anything clinical is passed to a person with the transcript, and urgent calls are escalated using the rules the practice sets.

Does it integrate with our practice management system?

Only where an integration can be connected and tested. Otherwise it books to your agreed process and leaves a complete record for staff. We do not describe an integration as live before it is.

Is it suitable for a small practice with one receptionist?

That is a common fit: it covers the phone while the desk is busy and after hours, and hands back anything that needs a person. It is not a replacement for reception staff.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the channels and on how quickly the practice can approve the information and the escalation rules. Website chat goes live first because it needs no phone number; phone and SMS follow once a provider is connected and tested.

What a clinic front desk actually spends its day on

Most front-desk work is not clinical. It is confirming opening hours, explaining where to park, checking whether a service is offered, rescheduling, and asking the same six questions. That work is repetitive, it arrives in bursts, and when the desk is busy it is the phone that goes unanswered — the one channel where the caller has already decided they want to book.

Concierge takes the routine part of that queue and records it, so the person at the desk can stay with the patient in front of them.

What it will and will not answer

It answers from information the practice approves:

  • Practical questions — hours, location, parking, accessibility, the services offered, how to prepare for a visit.
  • Appointment logistics — offering agreed times, booking, rescheduling within your rules, confirming what the patient should bring.
  • Administration — enrolment or intake questions, whether a referral is needed, and where to send documents.

It does not diagnose, interpret symptoms, discuss results, or give clinical advice. It is instructed to hand anything that sounds clinical, urgent or distressed to a person immediately. That line is written down, tested with your team, and reviewed before activation — not left to the model to infer.

Privacy in practice

Health enquiries are sensitive, so the deployment is deliberately plain about data:

  • Each practice has its own workspace; staff access is role-controlled.
  • Contact details are captured only when the caller wants a booking or a callback.
  • Recording asks the caller first, and calls that are not recorded say so.
  • Retention and any integration with your practice management system are agreed in writing before activation.
  • Transcripts stay with the enquiry, and the practice can export them.

We are not the practice's privacy officer and do not claim compliance on its behalf. We will tell you what the tool stores and for how long, so that your own assessment is based on facts. See privacy and security.

What setup looks like for a practice

We collect the approved public information, write the escalation rules with your team, define the appointment rules, run test conversations using your real questions, and only then enable the channel. If your practice management system cannot be connected safely, the assistant still books into your agreed process and leaves the record for staff — it does not pretend to be integrated.
