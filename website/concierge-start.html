What goes on your site first
Most setups start with website chat, because it needs no phone number and no carrier. This is the window your visitors see, shown here as it runs on trendibizz.com. It opens from a button in the corner of the page, greets the visitor, and answers from the pages and business information you approved. When it does not know, it says so and offers to pass the question on.
Every conversation carries a notice that the visitor is chatting with an AI, that it can make mistakes, and that conversations may be saved so your team can follow up. Recording is an opt-in checkbox, and audio is kept for 30 days. You read the conversations as they arrive, and the details a visitor leaves become an enquiry in the built-in CRM.
The window is added with one script tag, by you or your developer, or we send you the snippet. Email, SMS and phone come after, each once its provider is connected and tested.


Cost, term and what is not promised


Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum. Texts run through your paired smartphone (your normal mobile plan applies); any landline forwarding service is billed separately by your phone provider. Website chat is the fastest channel to start with because it needs no number and no carrier.


We do not promise leads, sales, bookings or rankings. What the service sells is coverage: enquiries answered, captured and recorded instead of missed. If your volume does not justify it, we will say so. For the detail behind the price, read what an AI receptionist costs, and for the channel list see the AI receptionist and AI office overview.
Questions
How long does setup take?
We handle the full setup, configuration and end-to-end testing with you before anything goes live; website chat usually goes live first once your information is approved, followed by each configured channel.
Do I need to give you my website password?
No. Chat is installed with one script tag by you or your developer, or we send the snippet. Account connections happen in your own portal with your permission, and nothing is asked for by email.
Can I cancel the 3-month minimum?
The minimum term is what pays for setup and testing, so it is not refundable. After the first three months the plan renews monthly. Ask for the terms in writing before you commit.
