AI receptionist and AI office questions

Can an AI receptionist or AI office book property viewings?

Yes, for agreed times and rules. It offers viewing times, books the appointment and keeps it with the enquiry. You choose availability and appointment length.

Does it know my listings?

It answers from the public pages and listing information you load into its knowledge. If the answer is not there, it offers to pass the question to the team.

Does it replace an agent?

No. It handles routine enquiries, capture and booking, and passes negotiations and complex questions to a person.

How much does it cost?

USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum. Texts run through your paired smartphone (your normal mobile plan applies); any landline provider charges are separate.

Does it guarantee more listings?

No. It helps make sure enquiries are answered and recorded. We do not promise leads, listings or sales.

Can the assistant book viewings directly into my calendar?

It offers only the times you enable and keeps the booking with the enquiry. Whether it writes into a third-party calendar depends on the integrations agreed during setup, and each one is tested before activation.

Does it know which listings are still available?

Only from the source you approve, refreshed as part of setup. It is configured not to promise availability it cannot verify.

Can vendors use it too?

Yes, with role-controlled access. Enquiries, transcripts and bookings are shared where you allow it, and the agency controls who sees what.

What happens with finance or negotiation questions?

They are handed to a person with the transcript attached. The assistant does not discuss finance options, offer a price or negotiate.

Where property enquiries are lost

Two patterns repeat. The first is timing: a viewing request at 9pm gets answered the next afternoon, by which time the buyer has seen the property with another agency. The second is triage: a stream of enquiries that all look urgent, none of which state budget, area or timing, so the agent spends the morning qualifying instead of selling.

Concierge is configured to fix both, answering while the interest is fresh, and asking the four questions an agent would ask anyway, so the callback is a conversation rather than an interview.

What a property enquiry looks like when it is captured properly

  • Which property, address or listing reference, and whether they want to buy or rent.
  • Buying position, pre-approved, cash, or still exploring. Asking this politely early saves everyone time.
  • Timing, from "just looking" to "need to move next month".
  • Contact and consent, name, number, email, and permission to follow up.
  • The appointment, a viewing or a call booked into the slots you allow.

The record goes into your CRM with the transcript, so whoever calls back has the context and the history in one place.

Viewings, after hours and the handover

Concierge can offer viewing times that match your availability and the rules you set, for example, only properties that are vacant, and only within the hours you or the owner can attend. Anything that moves into negotiation, price, finance or the property's condition is passed to a person with the transcript. It does not describe a property beyond the approved public information, and it does not promise that a listing is still available without checking the source you nominate.

If your enquiries come mostly through a portal rather than your website, the same capture rules apply, see the AI receptionist and AI office overview for the channel list and the cost page for what each channel adds.

Privacy, consent and the vendor relationship

Property enquiries carry personal information, names, sometimes finance details, so the deployment is set up accordingly: each office has its own private workspace, access is role-controlled, recording asks the caller first, and retention is agreed rather than assumed. Where a vendor relationship exists, the agency controls who can see the enquiry and can export the record. See privacy and security for how data is stored and who can reach it.
