What penetration testing is

Penetration testing is a planned attempt to find and prove security weaknesses in a system you own or are authorised to test. The work follows an agreed scope, timing, and rules of engagement. You get written findings: what was tried, what worked, how serious it looks, and what to fix first.

It is not the same as a marketing scan that only lists open ports. It is also not an open-ended "red team" show with no written limit. We keep the scope honest so you know what was and was not covered.

Standards we cite

Engagements are informed by publicly available testing guidance. We cite these as reference frameworks, not as a claim of certification or compliance outcome:

  • OWASP Web Security Testing Guide (WSTG)
  • OWASP Top 10
  • NIST SP 800-115 (Technical Guide to Information Security Testing and Assessment, published 2008; still widely referenced; treat dated guidance carefully)

What we offer

Typical engagements for business websites and apps include:

  • External web application testing against an agreed host or staging URL
  • Checks for common and higher-impact issues such as auth flaws, injection, access-control mistakes, sensitive data exposure, and unsafe file or admin paths
  • A written report in plain English, with severity and fix guidance you can hand to a developer
  • A short debrief call if you want one, to walk through the findings

We only test systems you are allowed to have tested. You confirm ownership or written authorisation before anything starts.

What we do not claim

  • No "hack-proof" or "fully secure" guarantee after the test
  • No fake enterprise theatre. If a big compliance pen test or certified third-party report is what you need, we say so
  • No destructive attacks outside the agreed rules
  • No social engineering or physical security unless that is scoped and priced separately in writing
  • No fixed public price on this page. Quotes are on enquiry

How this differs from our website security check

The website security check is a lighter, practical pass. It looks for exposed admin paths, common misconfigurations, and everyday web risks, then lists findings. Penetration testing goes further inside an agreed scope and tries harder to prove exploitable issues. Pick the light check when you want a quick hygiene pass. Pick penetration testing when you want a scoped test with a formal quote.

FAQ

Is penetration testing the same as your website security check?

No. The security check is a light practical pass. Penetration testing is a separate, scoped engagement. Ask which fits your risk and budget.

Do you publish a fixed price?

No. Quotes are on enquiry in USD after scope is agreed.

Will a pen test make my site hack-proof?

No. A test finds issues inside the agreed scope at a point in time. It does not prove lasting safety, and it is not a compliance certificate by itself.

Who performs the tests, and what credentials do you hold?

TRENDI LIMITED delivers scoped external web-app testing as described on this page. We do not publish CREST, OSCP, or similar marks here. Partner delivery, if any, is named in the written quote with referral-fee disclosure.

Which standards do you follow?

We use OWASP WSTG and the OWASP Top 10 as practical reference, and we note NIST SP 800-115 (2008) as background guidance. Citing a standard is not the same as accreditation or a compliance certificate.

Do I need written authorisation?

Yes. You confirm ownership or written authorisation before testing starts. Our Terms require written authorisation and an agreed scope / rules of engagement. Have your own legal adviser review any statement of work before relying on it.

Is this part of the monthly SEO package?

No. SEO and AI visibility are separate. Penetration testing is quoted on its own.
