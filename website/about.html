SEO and AI visibility

Trendi is a done-for-you SEO and AI-visibility service for English-speaking businesses worldwide. We split the work into three jobs. Indexed means Google and Bing have crawled your pages and chosen to store them. Found means those pages appear in search results and earn impressions and clicks. Mentioned means an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Perplexity names your business in an answer. We track mentions and links separately, because being named and being linked to are different outcomes. Each job has its own evidence and its own report line. We work with clients in any market where they sell, including worldwide, and nothing in this service is guaranteed.

Indexed

Search engines can reach, read and store your pages. We check crawl access, sitemaps and index signals.

Found

Your business is easier to find for the searches customers use. We map buyer questions to clear, useful pages.

Mentioned

Your business is shown accurately on the sources AI tools draw from. We check facts and track mentions and links separately.

How we work

We agree scope and access before any work starts, so both sides know what Trendi will touch. A person checks each change, and we check facts before they appear on a page. We use authentic third-party sources only. Every week we send a short report showing what changed and what we observed, with dates. If something does not work, the report says so. We never name or disclose our clients, so we cannot show you client results. The proof of our method is our own sample report on trendibizz.com, where the numbers, dates and sources are public, including the starting point.

What we won't do

Trendi does not promise rankings, traffic or AI mentions. Search engines and AI vendors make those decisions, not us. We do not invent reviews, testimonials, case studies or corroboration, and we do not place mentions that real people did not earn. The monthly SEO service covers the agreed search and visibility work. Concierge and penetration testing have separate scopes and terms. Honesty matters more in this field than most, because the results are hard to see from outside, and a report you cannot trust is worse than no report at all.
