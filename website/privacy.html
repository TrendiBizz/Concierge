Privacy

This page explains how Trendi (operated from New Zealand via trendibizz.com) handles personal information. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who we are

Trendi provides Concierge, SEO and AI visibility, and security review services. Contact: admin@trendibizz.com.

What we collect

  • If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, website and whatever you tell us about your business.
  • If you buy, Stripe collects your payment details and gives us your name, email and payment status. We never see or store your card number.
  • Our hosting and email providers may see technical details such as IP address and browser type when you visit the site or send mail.
  • If you enter a referral code on the contact form, we keep it with your enquiry only to credit the partner who referred you.
  • If you join our referral partner programme, we keep the payout details you give us only to pay your referral payments, and share them only with the payment service you chose.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and deliver the service.
  • To invoice you and meet our tax obligations.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your information.

Who else sees it

We host Concierge and this website on DigitalOcean (Singapore region) behind Cloudflare edge routing. Sub-processors involved in delivering the service include DigitalOcean (hosting and database), Cloudflare (edge proxy, DNS and inbound email routing), xAI (AI text and voice responses), Brevo (outbound email delivery), Stripe (payment processing), Google Calendar (only when a business connects its calendar), and connected phone/landline or paired-phone SMS services where activated. They may process data outside New Zealand. We only share what is needed to run the service or when the law requires it.

Concierge connected accounts and AI

If you connect a social account to Concierge, we store the account identifier, granted permissions and encrypted access credentials for your business workspace. We use them to identify your publishing destinations and deliver posts you approve. Drafts, uploaded images or videos, approval records and publishing results are stored with your workspace.

Concierge sends your supplied business brief to xAI to prepare editable social drafts. Connected-account content is not retrieved for that draft adaptation. Website conversations and voice interactions use xAI to generate responses. Connected telephone, email and SMS services process the communications needed for those channels when they are activated.

Approved posts and selected media are sent to the social networks you choose. The networks process that information under their own policies. Disconnecting a social account erases its local access credentials; it does not delete posts already published on the network. Historical account and approval records may remain in the workspace and backups. To request access, correction or deletion of Concierge information, email admin@trendibizz.com.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies, and Google Analytics, which shows how visitors find and use the site. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (such as _ga) and records the pages viewed, the referring site, device and browser type, and approximate location. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses, and we do not send names, email addresses or form contents to it. We have not linked it to any advertising account, and we do not set cookies for ads. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, or block it with Google's opt-out browser add-on. If this changes, we will update this page.

Email delivery and link tracking

We send email through Brevo. Brevo records whether each message was delivered and may record when it is opened or a link in it is clicked. It reports those events to us so we can see whether our mail is arriving.

Links in emails we send to businesses that use Concierge, such as account invitations and alerts, in campaign emails, and in email replies Concierge sends on a business's behalf, may pass through concierge.trendibizz.com/studio/l/… before opening the page they point to. We record the first click on each link with the time, the link, the IP address and the browser type, so we and the business that sent the email can see it was acted on. Invoices and automatic acknowledgements sent to a business's customers do not use these links.

How long we keep it

Voice recordings in Concierge are kept for 30 days by default and then automatically purged. Enquiry records, transcripts, emails and workspace data are kept while your account is active or as agreed with the business, and can be erased on request through your workspace or by emailing admin@trendibizz.com. Invoices and tax records are kept for as long as required under New Zealand tax law.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where we are allowed to. Email admin@trendibizz.com. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

We may update this page. The version on this site is the current one.
