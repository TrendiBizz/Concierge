Privacy

This page explains how Trendi (operated from worldwide via trendibizz.com) handles personal information. We follow the worldwide Privacy Act 2020.

Who we are

Trendi provides Concierge, SEO and AI visibility, and security review services. Contact: admin@trendibizz.com.

What we collect

  • If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, website and whatever you tell us about your business.
  • If you buy, Stripe collects your payment details and gives us your name, email and payment status. We never see or store your card number.
  • Our hosting and email providers may see technical details such as IP address and browser type when you visit the site or send mail.
  • If you enter a referral code on the contact form, we keep it with your enquiry only to credit the partner who referred you.
  • If you join our referral partner programme, we keep the payout details you give us only to pay your referral payments, and share them only with the payment service you chose.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and deliver the service.
  • To invoice you and meet our tax obligations.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your information.

Who else sees it

Stripe processes payments. Our email and hosting providers handle email and the website. They may store data outside my country. We only share what is needed to run the service or when the law requires it.

Concierge connected accounts and AI

If you connect a social account to Concierge, we store the account identifier, granted permissions and encrypted access credentials for your business workspace. We use them to identify your publishing destinations and deliver posts you approve. Drafts, uploaded images or videos, approval records and publishing results are stored with your workspace.

Concierge sends your supplied business brief to xAI to prepare editable social drafts. Connected-account content is not retrieved for that draft adaptation. Website conversations and voice interactions use xAI to generate responses. Connected telephone, email and SMS services process the communications needed for those channels when they are activated.

Approved posts and selected media are sent to the social networks you choose. The networks process that information under their own policies. Disconnecting a social account erases its local access credentials; it does not delete posts already published on the network. Historical account and approval records may remain in the workspace and backups. To request access, correction or deletion of Concierge information, email admin@trendibizz.com.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not track you for advertising. We do not set first-party cookies for ads. If that changes, we will update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep emails and invoices for as long as we need them to deliver the service and meet legal and tax requirements, then we delete or archive them.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where we are allowed to. Email admin@trendibizz.com. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

We may update this page. The version on this site is the current one.
