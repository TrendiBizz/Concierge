The chat window, as it looks on this site

The window opens from a button in the corner of the page. It shows who the visitor is talking to, a greeting written for the business and a box to type a question. Two lines stay under the box in every conversation: an optional checkbox to record the conversation for the business, with audio kept for 30 days, and a plain notice that the visitor is chatting with an AI, that it can make mistakes, and that conversations may be saved so the team can follow up.

That notice is there on purpose. Visitors should know they are not talking to a person, and you should be able to read what was said. Answers come from the pages and business information you approve. When the answer is not there, the assistant says so and offers to pass the question on instead of guessing.

On this site the window also has a microphone button for a voice conversation in the browser. It only starts after the visitor allows microphone access, and if voice drops the visitor can keep typing. Visitors can ask for a copy of the conversation, and the window tells them when that copy is queued rather than claiming it has arrived.

How setup works, step by step

  1. Send a setup request

    Fill in the short form on the setup page: business name, website, a contact email, the channels you want and the plan. It prepares an email for you to review and send. Sending it does not create a subscription or switch anything on.

  2. A person reads it and replies

    We check the request against your website and reply with what is realistic for the channels you picked, what it costs and what we need from you first.

  3. You get a private portal

    An invitation lets you set your password and connect the accounts you approve. We never ask for the password to your website, your phone system or your inbox.

  4. We load and test it

    We read your approved public pages into its knowledge, write down the rules (what it answers, what goes to a person, who to reach when something is urgent) and try the questions your customers ask.

  5. You review, then it goes live

    You see the greeting, the handover rules and a set of real test conversations. One line of code adds the chat window, added by you or your developer, or we send you the snippet. Each additional channel is tested before activation.

Website chat usually goes live first once your information is approved, and each additional channel is configured and tested end-to-end with you before it goes live. Anything that cannot be tested is said out loud rather than switched on untested.

Your existing CRM

If you already use one, we agree how to connect it before you start and test it first. If you do not, Concierge keeps its own built-in CRM.

Social posting (included)

Social posting is part of Concierge and included in the price. Your own business accounts, linked in your portal; you prepare and approve posts and Concierge publishes them. Publishing is switched on per network after platform approval where required; X needs funded API access and TikTok needs Content Posting approval.

Before any channel, we also need to know which enquiries a person must handle, the order of people to reach when something is urgent, and your retention and consent choices for stored messages.

What it does not do

  • It answers in English.
  • It can make mistakes. That is why the notice sits under every conversation, why it is set up to admit a gap rather than guess, and why you can read every conversation.
  • Phone, SMS and email work only once a provider is connected and tested. What is possible differs by country and provider.
  • It does not take payments. Payment handoff or checkout has to be agreed and verified first; the current integration has not been certified to collect payments directly.
  • Voicemail is available now: a caller who leaves one is recorded, added to your CRM as an enquiry and you are alerted. It is not a replacement for a person on urgent matters.
  • It is the wrong buy if most of your enquiries are complex, bespoke or high value. A human answering service is likely the better fit, and we will tell you so.
  • It does not promise leads, sales, bookings or rankings. It makes sure enquiries are answered and recorded instead of missed.

AI receptionist compared with a human answering service

Availability

Concierge can answer every hour you enable it, including outside office hours. A human service needs staffing or on-call arrangements.

Cost

Concierge is a fixed monthly subscription, not a per-call charge. Human answering services usually price per call or per minute.

Complex conversations

People are better at negotiation, nuance and difficult calls. Concierge is designed for routine answering, capture and booking, and should hand over when a person is needed.

Concierge is an assistant, not a replacement for every human role. We do not promise a reduction in headcount or costs.

Voicemail, and the software you already use

Voicemail is available now. A caller who leaves a message is recorded, added to your CRM as an enquiry, and you are alerted. Concierge can also pass enquiries and bookings on to the tools you already use.

  • ServiceM8: an enquiry can create a job
  • Xero: customers can be sent to Xero as contacts
  • Microsoft 365 calendar: a booked appointment can be added to your calendar
  • Webhooks: signed events sent to your own system when enquiries and bookings happen

Each connection uses your own account and permission, and is tested with you before it is switched on.

What is an AI receptionist or AI office?

An AI office answers routine customer enquiries from channels such as website chat, email, SMS and phone. Concierge answers from your own business information, captures contact details, offers agreed appointment times and records everything in a CRM for your team.

How much does an AI office cost?

Trendi Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, then it renews monthly. The USD $199 a month Concierge price includes everything in the Concierge offer: full setup and testing, your dedicated business phone number, voice answering, SMS and email confirmations, voicemail, the built-in CRM, integrations and social posting. There are no extras. SEO and AI visibility and penetration testing are separate products, priced separately. Prices are in USD. We do not promise leads or sales.

Does it answer phone calls?

It can answer your business phone number, take a message and record the enquiry. Trendi provisions and configures the number for you as part of setup, and each channel is tested with you before activation.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, for agreed appointment times and hours. Concierge offers times, books the appointment and keeps the booking with the enquiry. You choose the hours, appointment length and rules.

Does it replace reception staff?

No. It handles routine answering, capture and booking, and hands complex or sensitive conversations to a person with the context. It is an assistant, not a replacement for every human role.

Does it work outside your country?

Yes. Trendi works with businesses worldwide. The assistant answers in English, uses your business hours and timezone, and the channels available depend on the providers that operate in your country.

Is my business data private?

Each business has its own private CRM workspace. Enquiries, contacts and conversations are kept for that business. Access is controlled by owner, editor and viewer roles. Ask us about data retention and any integration before you start.

Does an AI office guarantee more leads?

No. It helps make sure enquiries are answered and recorded rather than missed. How many customers arrive and what they decide is outside our control, and we do not promise leads, sales or rankings.

What do I need ready before setup?

Your website and the pages that may be used, your services, opening hours and service area, the answers to the questions customers ask most, which enquiries a person must handle, and appointment rules if you want bookings. For SMS you also need a paired smartphone. Trendi sets up your phone number for you.

How long does setup take?

We handle the full setup, configuration and end-to-end testing with you before anything goes live; website chat usually goes live first once your information is approved, followed by each configured channel.

Do you need my website or inbox password?

No. Chat is added with one script tag by you or your developer, or we send the snippet. Account connections happen in your own portal with your permission.
