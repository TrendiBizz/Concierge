AI receptionist and AI office questions

Can an AI receptionist or AI office handle builder enquiries?

It answers routine questions, captures project details and books agreed site visits. Complex or commercial conversations are passed to you with the context.

Can it take plan drawings?

Your team can receive plans through the Concierge design upload link, and an IFC model can provide wall areas for cladding quotes. PDF and DWG plans are reviewed by a person.

Does it price the job?

No. It captures the enquiry and details. Pricing and the quotation are reviewed and signed off by the business owner.

Does it work after hours?

Yes, for the hours you enable. Phone and SMS channels require providers and testing.

How much does it cost?

USD $250 a month with a 3-month minimum, then monthly. Provider charges for phone and SMS are separate.

Can it handle calls while I am on site?

Yes, inside the hours you enable. A connected number is answered, the enquiry is captured in the customer’s own words, and a callback or site visit is offered from the times you allow. It does not price the job.

What happens to plans and drawings it receives?

They arrive through the upload link and stay with the enquiry in your workspace, where your team reviews them. PDF and DWG plans are reviewed by a person; an IFC model can supply wall areas for cladding measurement.

Does it work for emergency callouts?

Only with clear escalation rules, and it is not a safety system. If most of your work is urgent, a person answering the phone is the better arrangement. We tell you that before you sign up rather than after.

Can it quote from my price list?

It can state the rules and inclusions you approve, such as callout fees or minimum job size. It does not produce a quotation: that stays with you.

How a trade enquiry actually arrives

It rarely arrives as a clean form. It arrives as a call while you are on a roof, a message at 7am from someone who found you at 11pm, or a photo of a plan with two sentences attached. What decides whether you win the job is not the quote — it is whether the enquiry exists in a usable form by the time you sit down with a coffee. Most trades lose jobs to silence rather than price.

Concierge is set up to keep the enquiry intact: who it is, how to reach them, what the job is, roughly how big, and when they want it done. Then it books the site visit or the callback into a slot you allow.

What it captures on a first contact

  • The job type — new build, renovation, repair, maintenance, or something that needs a person immediately.
  • Location and access — the suburb or area, so you can judge travel before you ring back.
  • Scope in the customer's words — a transcript beats a summary written from memory.
  • Timing — when they want it started, and whether it is urgent.
  • Contact details with permission — name, number, email, and consent to follow up.
  • Attachments — plans or photos through the upload link, and an IFC model where one exists so wall areas can be measured for cladding quotes.

It does not price the job. Every quotation is reviewed and signed off by you, which is exactly where the judgement belongs.

The site-visit booking, without the phone tag

The usual failure is not the first call, it is the third: three messages to agree a time, by which point the customer has had two other quotes. An assistant that offers only times you have enabled, with your travel buffer, removes that loop. You keep the diary; it does the asking. If nothing fits, the enquiry is recorded with a preferred window and your team rings back once.

For the wider picture of what the assistant does across channels, read the AI receptionist and AI office overview, and for the cost comparison see what an AI receptionist costs.

What it does not do, on purpose

It does not quote, negotiate, approve variations, or promise a start date that is not in your diary. It does not give building advice or interpret plans. Anything that sounds like a dispute, a defect claim or a safety issue is escalated to a person with the transcript attached. If your work is mostly emergency callouts, a human answering arrangement is still the better fit and we will say so before you sign up.
