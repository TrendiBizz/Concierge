SEO pricing models: monthly retainer, project or hourly

SEO pricing models usually come in three kinds. A monthly retainer is a set fee each month for ongoing work, which suits SEO because it is rarely a one-off job. Project pricing is a fixed fee for a defined piece of work, such as an audit or a set of page rewrites. Hourly pricing bills for time spent, which can suit advice or small tasks but is harder to budget for. Each can work, depending on your goals. Trendi uses one monthly retainer, so your monthly SEO cost is clear from the start. See what's included on this page.
