A Smith.ai alternative for any business

Smith.ai sells AI and human receptionist plans billed per call. Concierge is an AI assistant for phone, chat, email and SMS at one monthly price. This page says where each fits.

The short version

Choose Smith.ai if you want real people answering your calls, or a way to move a call to a live receptionist. Choose Concierge if you want an AI assistant covering several channels for a flat monthly price, set up and tested for you.

Price, as published

From Smith.ai's own pricing pages (checked 25 November 2026): its AI Receptionist has a free plan of 25 calls a month, then $150 a month for 75 calls and $500 for 300 calls, with overage per call. Its human virtual receptionist plans are $300 a month for 30 calls, $810 for 90 and $2,100 for 300. Billing is per call, month to month. See smith.ai/pricing for current figures.

Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, including setup, testing, a business phone number, voice answering, SMS and email confirmations, voicemail, CRM and integrations. Provider charges for calls and SMS are separate.

Where Smith.ai fits better

Smith.ai offers receptionists who are real people, answering 24/7, and its AI plans can pass a call to a live receptionist. It also has a free tier, month-to-month terms, and a 30-day money-back guarantee on its human plans. Concierge has a 3-month minimum.

What Concierge adds

One price whatever the call volume, with chat, email and SMS handled alongside the phone, and one conversation history per customer. At a high number of calls a flat price can cost less than per-call billing; at a low number it can cost more.

Questions

Is Concierge a Smith.ai replacement?

For AI call answering, capturing enquiries and booking, yes. If you want a person on the phone, Smith.ai's human plans do that; Concierge is an AI assistant.

How much does Smith.ai cost compared with Concierge?

Smith.ai's monthly AI plans cost $0, $150 and $500, by calls included, and its human plans $300 to $2,100 a month (checked 25 November 2026). Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum.

Which is cheaper?

It depends on how many calls you get. Under about 75 calls a month, Smith.ai's AI Pro plan costs less. Count your calls first.
