Questions before you start

What is Concierge?

Concierge is an assistant for customer enquiries and office work. It answers visitors from your own pages, captures their contact details and can offer appointment times.

Where does it get its answers?

From the pages on your website, which we load into its knowledge base. If the answer is not there, it says so and offers to take the visitor's details instead of guessing.

Can it answer the phone?

Concierge is built to handle telephone enquiries as well as website chat, email and SMS. Each channel is set up and tested with its provider before it is switched on for a customer.

What does Concierge cost?

Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, then it renews monthly.

Is it set up for my business?

Yes. We set Concierge up around your business: your own phone number, text and email details, your opening hours, and answers from your own pages. Each channel is tested before it is switched on.

Can it connect to my CRM and email?

Yes. Concierge has its own built-in CRM. If you also use another CRM or email system, we agree and test that integration before activation. Concierge includes a built-in CRM that captures enquiries from your connected calls, emails, texts and website chat, with contact details, conversation history and follow-up status. The Trendi assistant and its core features are the same for every customer.

How will I know what Concierge recorded?

Enquiries from your connected channels are recorded in the built-in CRM. Concierge can answer by email, text or call when needed, using the channels and approval rules you enable. You choose how your team is notified. Voicemail is available now: a recorded message is added to your CRM as an enquiry and you are alerted. Nothing is sent to you or your customers that you did not choose.

Does it promise leads or sales?

No. Concierge answers and records what visitors ask and enter. How many visitors use it, and what they decide, is up to them.

Can I use SEO or a security review as well?

Yes. SEO and AI visibility and penetration testing are separate services, each with its own page. One set of terms covers every Trendi service. Use any one, or more than one.
