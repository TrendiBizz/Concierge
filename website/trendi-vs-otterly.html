Is Trendi an Otterly alternative?

Partly. Only if you want the work done for you. Otterly is software. Trendi is a service. If you want a dashboard and plan to act on it yourself, Otterly is built for that. If you want someone else to do the fixes, the articles and the checks, that is what we do.

The two do different jobs. A tool shows you what AI engines say. A service changes the pages and sources behind those answers. Some buyers use both. We make no claim about what happens when they do.

Done-for-you vs AI search monitoring software

What Otterly is

Otterly describes itself as an "AI Search monitoring and optimization platform". Its home page lists Prompt Research, AI Search Analytics, Content Audit and GEO Optimization among its features. Its pricing page lists unlimited brand reports.

It is self-serve. You set it up, you read the results and you act on the recommendations yourself.

What Trendi is

Trendi is a done-for-you service. We do the work and send a short report every week. Trendi costs USD $1,500 per month, with a three-month minimum, then month to month.

A USD $1,000 deposit secures a start date and is credited against the first invoice. The service covers three things: Indexed (search engines can reach, read and store your pages), Found (pages are easier to find for the searches customers use) and Mentioned (accurate information on the sources AI tools draw from, so an answer may name you). You can read more on our AI visibility page.

  • AI mention tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, with mentions and links logged separately
  • Third-party corroboration: directories, listicles, reviews and press targets, authentic only
  • Keyword research and a search map
  • In-depth articles each month, plus comparison and alternatives pages
  • Page fixes: titles, copy, speed and internal links
  • Index checks in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
  • Fact checks so prices and features are right everywhere, and a short report every week

What does each cost?

Otterly's pricing page lists four plans, in USD per month on monthly billing: Lite at $29 for 15 search prompts, Standard at $189 for 100, Premium at $489 for 400, and Enterprise at a custom price from 1,000 prompts. Annual billing is listed as 10% off when paid in full, which works out to $25, $160 and $422 per month for the first three plans.

Every plan tracks four engines. Google AI Mode, Gemini and Claude are paid add-ons. Google AI Mode, for example, is listed at $9, $59 or $149 per month depending on the plan. Extra prompts are listed at $99 per 100 per month on Standard and Premium, and are not available on Lite.

For a cost reference on another tool, Profound's pricing page shows a trial with no price and a custom-priced Enterprise plan. No Profound price is published, so we do not estimate one. See Trendi vs Profound for that comparison.

A software price pays for the tool. Someone still has to do the work the tool points to. Trendi's price covers the work. We do not claim either is cheaper. The right comparison is your own time and budget against what you want done. Our pricing page lists the full scope.

Trendi tracks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. Mentions and links are logged separately, and you get a short report every week. We measure success by whether you appear in a fixed prompt set you chose, and by movement in the Search Console queries you care about.

  • You want to track AI search yourself, and a plan starting at USD $29 a month on Otterly's pricing page suits your budget
