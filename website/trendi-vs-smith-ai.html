Concierge by Trendi vs Smith.ai: pricing and setup

By Trendi · Published and sources checked 20 November 2026

Smith.ai publishes separate AI, hybrid and human receptionist offerings. This comparison uses its AI Receptionist pricing, so a human-receptionist price is not presented as the cost of its AI product.

Disclosure: this page is written by Trendi, which sells Concierge. We are not affiliated with Smith.ai. We compared public vendor pages and have not independently tested its call quality, reliability or customer outcomes. Prices and features can change.

Published pricing and scope

Its AI pricing page lists an entry plan with 25 calls per month and USD3 per additional call. Pro starts at USD150 per month for 75 calls; Enterprise starts at USD500 for 300 calls. The page lists scheduling and integrations, with more setup support at higher tiers. Smith.ai pricing source.

Smith.ai's entry AI plan includes 25 calls per month; Concierge starts at USD250/month with a three-month minimum. Compare the actual channels and setup scope you need, then model expected usage and additional charges. Ask both vendors for the complete cost, including provider usage, numbers, messaging, integrations and any setup charges that apply to your proposed configuration.

What Concierge includes and what must be activated

Concierge by Trendi combines customer-enquiry assistance with a built-in CRM. Its scope covers email, website chat, phone and SMS. Email is live; phone and SMS depend on provider configuration and successful channel testing before activation. Do not assume every advertised channel is active on a new account.

The subscription starts at USD250 per month with a three-month minimum. The Concierge cost guide explains what is included and what is separate. Agree your phone region, contact details, handover rules and any booking connections before committing.

Which fits your business?

Compare the AI plan with hybrid or human escalation separately if callers need a person. Ask which call types use human support, how escalation is charged, and which integrations your chosen plan includes.

Consider Concierge when you want enquiry handling and a built-in CRM configured around your business. Ask us to show your exact workflow, including what happens when an answer is unknown, a customer asks for a person, or a provider is unavailable.

A practical test before switching

  • Try a normal enquiry, an unclear request and an urgent handover.
  • Check that contact details and the enquiry reach the right person or CRM record.
  • Confirm phone-number availability, call forwarding and messaging in your country.
  • Ask about recordings, retention, deletion and access to customer information.
  • Get the total recurring cost and cancellation terms in writing.

For trades, include a quote enquiry and a site-visit request in the test. Our builders and trades guide describes that workflow. For a broader checklist, read how to choose an AI receptionist.
