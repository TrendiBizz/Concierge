What a virtual receptionist does

A virtual receptionist answers the routine calls and messages a business receives, so the team can keep working. Concierge answers from your own business information, captures the caller’s name and contact details, offers agreed appointment times and records the conversation.

It is not a call centre and it is not a person. It handles routine questions and passes complex or sensitive conversations to your team with the context.

Built for worldwide

Concierge works in your timezone, with your opening hours and your local number. It can cover after-hours enquiries, weekends and busy periods. For phone calls, a local number and voice provider are required, and each channel is tested before activation.

It also works for businesses outside my country; the available channels depend on the providers in your country.

Virtual receptionist vs a human answering service

A human answering service charges per call, per minute or per agent hour. Concierge is a fixed monthly subscription, answers every hour you enable, and records the enquiry in your CRM. People remain better at negotiation, complaints and high-value conversations; the practical answer is often both.

Questions

What is a virtual receptionist?

A virtual receptionist answers routine customer calls and messages remotely. Concierge is an AI virtual receptionist that answers from your business information, captures contact details, books agreed appointments and records the enquiry in a CRM.

How much does a virtual receptionist cost?

Concierge is USD $400 a month with a 3-month minimum, then it renews monthly. After the first three months you can move to a yearly plan at USD $4,080. Phone and SMS provider charges are separate.

Does it work with a local phone number?

Yes, when a local number and voice provider are connected and tested. Website chat works without a number. Telephone availability depends on the provider and the region.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, for agreed times and hours. It offers times, books the appointment and keeps the booking with the enquiry. You choose the hours and appointment length.

Does it replace reception staff?

No. It handles routine answering, capture and booking, and hands complex or sensitive conversations to a person. It is an assistant, not a replacement for every role.

Does it guarantee more leads?

No. It helps make sure enquiries are answered and recorded rather than missed. We do not promise leads, sales or rankings.
