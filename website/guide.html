Set up Concierge piece by piece

Inside Studio’s Setup & Onboarding Hub, these 7 stages track your progress automatically and include live verification checks at every step, or our team can configure any stage with you.

  1. Stage 1, Business identity & approved knowledge (5 min)

    Enter your website URL and Concierge scans your public pages, logo, brand colour, and country timezone. Add plain-English notes for anything not on your site (call-out fees, service areas, policies). A built-in Price Guard stops Concierge from ever inventing a quote. Use the built-in Sandbox Test Chat to ask real customer questions in dry-run mode before going live.

  2. Stage 2, Owner alerts, takeover & pause controls (3 min)

    Set your primary notification email, optional fallback phone, and Slack or Microsoft Teams webhook. Concierge sends one deduplicated summary alert per enquiry, never a flood of duplicates. Send yourself a [TEST] alert email right from Stage 2. Learn your two instant safety switches: Per-Customer Takeover (reply once in Inbox and Concierge steps back for that customer) and the Global Pause Switch at the top of Studio.

  3. Stage 3, Website chat & browser voice (5 min)

    Copy your one-line widget script and follow the built-in guide for WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, or custom HTML, or click Have Trendi install it for me for hands-on installation. Every visitor sees a clear AI disclosure plus an optional microphone button for browser voice. Click Verify widget on my site to confirm the script is live and reachable.

  4. Stage 4, Bookings & two-way calendar sync (5 min)

    Set your weekly opening hours (including split shifts) and appointment length. Connect Google Calendar, Microsoft 365 Calendar, or subscribe from Apple/Outlook via your private ICS feed. Concierge checks your real availability, reads the exact time back before locking it in, sends a confirmation with a self-serve reschedule/cancel link, and schedules 24-hour and 2-hour reminders. Click Preview next 7 days’ slots to verify your availability immediately.

  5. Stage 5, Phone, voicemail & missed-call text-back (5 min)

    Trendi provisions and configures a dedicated business phone number tailored to your country and local timezone. Forward calls unconditionally, on busy/no-answer, or after hours. Callers hear a clear AI disclosure, voicemails are recorded and added straight to your CRM with an owner alert, and optional missed-call text-back sends one follow-up SMS with your booking link if a caller hangs up early.

  6. Stage 6, Email, SMS & connected business software (5 min)

    Enable inbound email as drafts for your review (recommended default) or verified auto-reply, and run the live Email Readiness Check. Pair your SMS gateway or connect Meta Messaging (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram DMs). Connect the software you already use, Xero (contacts), Microsoft 365 Calendar, ServiceM8 (jobs), or signed Webhooks & API keys for Zapier and Make (with a 1-click test event button).

  7. Stage 7, Go-live scorecard & daily Studio workflow (3 min)

    Review your live automated Readiness Scorecard across identity, knowledge, alerts, bookings, phone, and integrations. Once ready, click Launch Concierge and use your 4 daily Studio screens: Home (daily overview), Enquiries & Inbox (reply, score, stage, takeover), Contacts (one unified timeline per person), and Bookings & Quotes. Every screen in Studio also includes a ? Guide button so you can review how any feature works anytime.

How every enquiry flows through Concierge

  • Every channel captured: Website chat, browser voice, phone calls, voicemails, SMS, inbound email, and Meta DMs all enter one CRM.
  • One unified contact per customer: Repeat enquiries across phone, email, and chat merge onto the same customer timeline automatically.
  • You stay in control: Explicit per-channel permissions, instant human takeover, global pause switch, and full conversation transcripts.
  • Tailored worldwide: Setup is tailored to your country, phone format, timezone, and currency, with multi-language website and Studio support.
