What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the work of making a business easy for search engines and AI assistants to find, understand and mention. In practice that means getting pages indexed, publishing clear content and building third-party corroboration. For more detail, read about AI visibility and generative engine optimization.

Some providers call themselves an AI SEO agency. Trendi is a done-for-you service: we do the work, and you are not handed a tool to run.

What Trendi does, week by week

The exact order depends on what your site needs, and the scope is agreed before work starts. A typical start looks like this.

  1. Before week 1: You send one sentence: your website and a question customers ask. We reply with fit, access needs and a clear scope. Work starts once the scope is agreed.
  2. Week 1: Access and a starting point. We check index status in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, check crawl access and robots.txt rules, agree the fixed prompt set with you and record where things stand, with mentions and links logged separately. You get the first weekly report.
  3. Week 2: First fixes. Page fixes from the week 1 list (titles, copy, speed and internal links), keyword research and a search map, and fact checks on your prices and features.
  4. Weeks 3 and 4: First content and corroboration. The first in-depth article or comparison page, and a list of authentic third-party corroboration targets.
  5. Every week after that: More page fixes, articles and corroboration work, index checks, mention tracking on the same prompt set, and a short weekly report. After the three-month minimum, the service continues month to month.

This is a schedule of work, not of results. What search engines and AI assistants do with the work is up to them.

Why isn't my business showing in ChatGPT?

Work through three steps: Indexed, Found, Mentioned.

Indexed: can search engines reach, read and store your pages? If pages are blocked or not stored, nothing else follows. Check that AI crawlers are not blocked with the AI crawler robots.txt checker, and run the Site snapshot for a quick look at the basics.

Found: are your pages easy to find for the searches customers actually use? Clear titles and copy that answer those searches help.

Mentioned: is your business described accurately on the other sources AI tools draw from, such as directories, reviews and articles? If those sources are thin or wrong, an answer is less likely to name you. Our guide to getting mentioned in ChatGPT or Perplexity covers this in more depth.

ChatGPT answers are not a fixed ranked list. The same question can give different answers on different days. Fixing these steps makes your business easier to find and describe, but it does not guarantee a mention.

Indexed by Google but no traffic: what now?

Indexed is not the same as found. Indexed means Google has stored the page. Found means the page appears for the searches your customers type. Indexed is the first step, and Found is the next.

Open Search Console and look at which queries show impressions for your pages. If a page has none, it may not answer the searches customers use. If it has impressions but few clicks, the title and copy may need work.

Our note on how long indexing takes explains the first step, and the Site snapshot shows what to look at first. We do not promise traffic. We fix the pages and report what changes.
