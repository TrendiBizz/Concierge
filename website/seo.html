Asking AI isn't the same as being indexed

An AI mention does not prove Google has indexed your site. An assistant can answer from its training data, from search snippets, or from what other websites say about you, without ever having stored your pages. Indexing is a separate step. Google and Bing must crawl a page and then choose to keep it in their index, and neither is obliged to do so. The reverse also holds: a page can be indexed and still never be mentioned by an AI assistant. Trendi checks index status in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and reports it separately from what AI assistants say. That keeps each problem visible, so we fix the right one.

LESS SETUP. MORE SUPPORT.

Three separate jobs

Trendi splits the work into three separate jobs, because each one has its own evidence. Indexed is shown by index coverage: which of your pages Google and Bing have stored. Found is shown by search impressions and clicks in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. Mentioned is shown by a prompt log, where we record whether an AI assistant named your business and whether it linked to your site. We report the three separately because a gain in one can hide a gap in another, and a single blended score would tell you little about what to fix next. None of the three is guaranteed. Search engines and AI assistants decide for themselves, and Trendi reports what we observed, with dates.

AI visibility: how a business gets mentioned in ChatGPT and Perplexity

AI visibility is whether search engines and AI assistants can read, find and name a business. Trendi treats it as three jobs: Indexed, Found and Mentioned. Alphabet's investor presentation (3 Jun 2026) said “AI Overviews now have over 2.5 billion users each month”, yet a mention is not a click: Pew Research Center (22 Jul 2025) found Google users who saw an AI summary clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits, against 15% without one.

ChatGPT does not rank pages the way Google does. ChatGPT SEO and Perplexity SEO mean crawl readiness, pages that answer buyer questions, and corroboration on other real sites. Trendi tracks mentions and links separately in each market a client sells in. Read the AI visibility guide, how to get mentioned in ChatGPT or Perplexity, or our own sample report. Nobody controls what an AI assistant says, so Trendi does not promise mentions.
