AI phone answering for small business

Concierge answers your business phone with an AI assistant: it greets callers in your business name, answers from your own information, takes details and books, and leaves you a transcript.

Request setupSee plans and pricing

Channels are configured and tested before activation. We do not promise leads, sales or rankings.

How the phone is answered

You get a dedicated business phone number. When a call arrives in the hours you set, the assistant answers, finds out what the caller needs, and either resolves it or records it for you.

Every call produces a transcript and an enquiry in your CRM, with the caller number and time, so nothing depends on someone remembering to ring back.

Calls you miss anyway

If a call is not answered, for example because it was not forwarded or the line was down, an SMS follow-up can be configured with your provider. See missed-call text back for how that works and its limits.

What it is not

It is not a promise that every caller becomes a customer. Anything outside the rules you set is recorded for you to follow up.

Questions

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Ask us during setup. Whether an existing number can be forwarded or moved depends on your provider and country.

Does it work after hours?

Yes. You choose the hours. Many customers use it only outside opening hours or when the phone is busy.

Will callers know it is an AI?

The greeting wording, including how the assistant introduces itself, is agreed with you during setup.

What does it cost?

USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum. Call and SMS charges from the provider are separate.

Does it work in New Zealand?

Yes, Trendi is New Zealand based. Each channel is tested for your country before activation.
