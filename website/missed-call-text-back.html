Answer first, follow up second

Concierge is designed to answer your calls, website chat, email and SMS during the hours you enable. That reduces missed calls before any follow-up is needed.

Where a call is missed, an SMS follow-up can be configured with your messaging provider during setup. It is tested before activation; delivery depends on the provider.

What the follow-up contains

A short message that acknowledges the missed call and asks how you can help, with a link or a reply path. The reply is captured in your CRM with the original enquiry.

We do not send messages you have not approved, and we do not promise that every missed call becomes a customer.

Set up

A phone number, voice provider and SMS provider are required for this workflow. Each is configured and tested for your business and country. Website chat works without them.

Questions

What is missed-call text back?

It is an automatic SMS sent after a missed call, inviting the caller to reply or book. Concierge can configure this with your provider where supported.

Does Concierge answer calls?

Yes, when a number and voice provider are connected and tested. It answers routine questions, captures details and records the enquiry.

Is the text back automatic?

It can be configured to send after a missed call. Delivery depends on your phone and SMS providers, and it is tested before activation.

How much does it cost?

Concierge is USD $250 a month with a 3-month minimum. Provider charges for calls and SMS are separate.

Does it guarantee the caller will reply?

No. It gives the caller an easy way back to your business, but we do not promise replies, leads or sales.
