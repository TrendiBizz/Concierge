Questions

What is missed-call text back?

It is an automatic SMS sent after a missed call, inviting the caller to reply or book. Concierge can configure this with your provider where supported.

Does Concierge answer calls?

Yes, when a number and voice provider are connected and tested. It answers routine questions, captures details and records the enquiry.

Is the text back automatic?

It can be configured to send after a missed call. Delivery depends on your phone line and paired smartphone, and it is tested before activation.

How much does it cost?

Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum. Provider charges for calls and SMS are separate.

Does it guarantee the caller will reply?

No. It gives the caller an easy way back to your business, but we do not promise replies, leads or sales.

Is a text back after a missed call allowed?

It depends on your market and how the customer gave their number. The message identifies your business, says why it is texting, allows an opt-out, and is sent by your provider. We agree the wording and the number with you before activation, and we do not enable it where we cannot test it.

How quickly should the follow-up go out?

Fast enough to be useful, the same working day, ideally within minutes. A reply that arrives hours later reads as a marketing blast rather than a response to a call.

What if the customer replies to the text?

The reply lands in your workspace with the original call, so a person can take it from there. Concierge does not keep texting indefinitely; the conversation is handed to your team.

Does it work with any phone provider?

No. Text-back needs a messaging provider that supports the sending number in your country. Website chat works without one, and we tell you before you pay if a channel cannot be tested in your market.

Why a missed call behaves differently from a missed form

Someone who fills in a form and gets silence will usually try again or wait. Someone who calls and gets no answer is standing there with a decision to make, so they make it: they call the next business. Nothing in your analytics records it, because the call never became a visit you could measure. That is why a small missed-call volume can look like a quiet week while the diary says otherwise.

A text back does not fix the missed call. It fixes the part you can still rescue: it gives the caller a way back in, in the same minute they were trying to reach you.

How the follow-up works

  1. The call is answered first. Concierge picks up within the channel rules you set, so a "missed call" here means a call that could not be completed, after hours, on a busy line, or outside the numbers you enabled.
  2. The enquiry is recorded. Caller number, time, and what the assistant did or could not do.
  3. A message is sent where the messaging provider supports it, from your business number, naming the business and offering one clear next step.
  4. The reply comes back into the same place. If the customer answers, the conversation continues in your workspace next to the original call, not in a personal phone.
  5. Your team picks it up. Every follow-up has an owner and a next action, and appears in the CRM with the rest of the enquiry history.

What a good follow-up message contains

Short, specific and easy to answer from a lock screen:

  • Who is texting, the business name, spelled the way customers know it.
  • Why, "you called us just now and we could not pick up".
  • One action, a reply, a booking link, or a time to call back. Not three options.
  • An opt-out, and a clear statement that replying is optional.
  • A real mobile number, not a short code the customer cannot call back.

We draft this with you and test it against real replies before it goes live, because a badly worded follow-up reads as spam and undoes the goodwill of answering at all.
