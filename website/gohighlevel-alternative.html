A GoHighLevel alternative for small business

GoHighLevel is a broad marketing and CRM platform, built largely for agencies. Concierge is narrower: an AI receptionist and office assistant for one business. This page says where each fits.

The short version

Choose GoHighLevel if you run an agency that wants funnels, courses, white-label and resale in one platform and has the time to configure it. Choose Concierge if you are a trade, clinic, agency-free small business that wants calls, chat, email and SMS answered, enquiries captured and bookings made, set up and tested for you.

Price, as published

From GoHighLevel's own pricing page (checked 12 November 2026): Starter USD $97, Unlimited $297 and Founder $497 a month, with a 14-day trial. The subscription buys the platform; usage is metered separately through a wallet, for example premium workflow actions at $0.01 per execution, and its voice and conversation AI agents are billed on top of the plan. Check the current figures at gohighlevel.com/pricing, as vendors change them.

Concierge is one price, USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, including setup, testing, a business phone number, voice answering, SMS and email confirmations, voicemail, CRM and integrations. Provider charges for calls and SMS are separate.

What you do not get from Concierge

No funnel builder, no courses or communities, no white-label or resale mode and no affiliate manager. If you need those, GoHighLevel is the better fit and we will say so. We keep Concierge small on purpose so that the part that matters to a small business, answering and following up every enquiry, is the part that is set up properly.

Who sets it up

Concierge is configured and tested with you before activation: your services, hours, prices, questions, calendar and who is told when. You do not build workflows.

Questions

Is Concierge a GoHighLevel replacement?

For the receptionist, enquiry capture, follow-up and booking part, yes. For funnels, courses, communities and agency resale, no.

How much does GoHighLevel cost compared with Concierge?

GoHighLevel's published plans are USD $97, $297 and $497 a month plus metered usage (checked 12 November 2026). Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, with provider call and SMS charges separate.

Can I move from GoHighLevel to Concierge?

Your knowledge base is built by reading your website. Ask us what else can be moved in your case.

Does Concierge have a CRM?

Yes, a built-in CRM with enquiries, follow-ups and bookings, and one conversation history per customer across channels.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We do not promise leads, sales, rankings or AI mentions.
