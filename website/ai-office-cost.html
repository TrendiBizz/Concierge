AI receptionist cost compared with a human answering service

Fixed vs per call

Trendi is a fixed monthly subscription. Human answering services usually charge per call, per minute or per agent hour, which makes busy months more expensive.

Coverage

A fixed subscription covers the channels you enable without a per-contact fee. A human service may charge for every call or message handled.

Complex work

People are better at negotiation and difficult calls. Budget for human handover where it matters; Concierge is designed to route those conversations to your team.

Compare the total cost, including provider charges and the human time you keep, not just the headline fee.

How to work out your total cost

  1. Start with the subscription

    Concierge is USD $199 a month with a 3-month minimum, then it renews monthly. The least you commit to is three months, USD $597.

  2. Add provider charges for each channel

    Landline voicemail forwarding uses your landline provider; texts run through your own paired smartphone on your existing mobile plan. Ask the provider in your country for its current rates. We do not quote them, because they vary by country and are billed by the provider, not by us.

  3. Add anything else you want connected

    A paid CRM or calendar you already use, or email hosting if you do not have it yet.

  4. Count your own time

    Setup needs your input: the pages it may use, your hours and service area, the questions customers ask, who handles what, and your appointment rules.

  5. Compare it with what a missed enquiry costs you

    Not with a promised number of leads. We do not promise leads or sales; what the subscription pays for is enquiries answered and recorded instead of missed.

What the minimum term pays for

The 3-month minimum is what pays for setup and testing, which happen before there is much to show. In that time a person checks your request against your website, your approved public pages are loaded into the assistant’s knowledge, the rules are written down, and each channel is tested before it is switched on. You see the greeting, the handover rules and a set of real test conversations before anything goes live.

Website chat usually goes live first, within days of your information being approved, because it needs no number and no carrier. Phone and SMS take longer, since a provider in your country has to be connected and tested.

When we would tell you not to buy it

  • Your enquiry volume is low enough that you already answer everything yourself. If the volume does not justify it, we will say so.
  • Most of your enquiries are complex, bespoke or high value. A human answering service is likely the better buy.
  • You need payments taken inside the conversation. The current integration has not been certified to collect payments directly.
  • You need phone answering in a country where no provider can be connected and tested. We will say that before you commit, not after.

If the real problem is that too few people find your site, Concierge is not the fix. That is our separate SEO and AI visibility service.

Questions to ask any AI office provider about price

  1. What is the total cost at my volume? Subscription, plus per-minute or per-message provider charges, plus setup. Ask for a worked example at your own call and message volume.
  2. Which charges come from someone else? Phone numbers, voice minutes and SMS are usually billed by a provider, not the software company.
  3. What is the minimum term, and how do I leave? A three-month minimum is normal for a configured service; a lock-in with no exit is not.
  4. Is setup and testing included, or billed separately?
  5. How do I get my data out? Export should not be a support ticket.
