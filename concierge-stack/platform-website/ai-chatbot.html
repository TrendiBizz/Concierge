What you can do

Chat widget for any site

Add one snippet to your website or funnel and the widget appears.

Answers from your content

Responses come from the information you provide about your business.

Captures leads

Names, emails and phone numbers go straight to the CRM.

Hands over to people

When a visitor wants a human, your team picks up in the same inbox.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add the widget

    Copy the snippet onto your site, or switch it on inside a Concierge page.

  2. 2

    Give it your answers

    Add your services, policies and common questions.

  3. 3

    Go live and watch

    Read conversations in your inbox and improve the answers that missed.
