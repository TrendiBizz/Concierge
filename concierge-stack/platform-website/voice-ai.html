Voice AI — an AI receptionist that picks up every call

Voice AI answers calls, handles common questions, qualifies the caller and books an appointment, then saves the conversation to their contact record.

Get startedSee all features

What you can do

Answers day and night

Calls get picked up on the first ring, including evenings, weekends and busy afternoons.

Qualifies before it books

Ask the questions you would ask, and capture the answers on the contact.

Books into your calendar

Callers choose a time while they are still on the line.

Summaries and transcripts

Read what was said in seconds instead of listening to a recording.

How it works

  1. 1

    Teach it your business

    Add your services, hours, prices and the questions you want asked.

  2. 2

    Connect your number

    Route calls to the assistant all the time or only when your team is busy.

  3. 3

    Review and refine

    Read the transcripts, tighten the answers and let it handle more over time.

Questions

Will callers know it is an AI?

You write the greeting. We recommend telling callers they are speaking with an automated assistant, and some regions require it.

Can it pass a call to a person?

Yes. Set rules for when a call should transfer to a team member.
