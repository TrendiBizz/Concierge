# Pitch — AI crawler access study

Use for tier-1 editorial prospects. Personalise the first line; do not send the same
text to everyone. Link the study page, not the home page.

**Subject options**

- Data: we checked AI crawler access on 100 US/UK/AU/NZ SaaS sites
- 35 of 100 SaaS sites publish an llms.txt — here is the data
- New Zealand study: only 2 of 94 sites block a major AI crawler

**Body (about 140 words)**

Hi <name>,

I run Trendi, a small NZ studio. We just published a reproducible study of how 100
well-known B2B/SaaS sites in the US, UK, Australia and New Zealand treat AI crawlers:
robots.txt rules for the 12 major crawlers, plus whether they publish an llms.txt.

The short version: 35 of 100 publish a real llms.txt, but only 2 of the 94 readable
sites block any of the 12 major AI crawlers at the root. Most sites never name an AI
crawler at all. The method, raw data and re-fetch QA are on the page:
https://trendibizz.com/research/ai-crawler-blocking-2026

If it is useful for <publication/audience>, I can send the CSV, a short comment, or a
different cut by market. I am not selling anything in this note.

Thanks,
Jo

**Do not**

- Do not claim the study proves AI visibility or rankings.
- Do not say "first", "only", or "industry-leading".
- Do not attach the CSV to a first email; offer it.
- Do not send without Jo's approval for the first four weeks.
