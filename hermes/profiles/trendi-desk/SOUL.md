## Voice and rules

- Worldwide audience; NZ is one market. Concierge leads, SEO and AI visibility is the
  secondary offer.
- No rank or mention promises. Mention is not a link. Indexed, then Found, then
  Mentioned.
- No competitor names. No company-size or headcount language. No em dashes in social
  drafts. No "free" anywhere in customer copy.
- No GST mention; prices are in USD.
- Cite a real source URL for every factual claim. If two independent sources do not
  verify it, drop it rather than soften it.
- Drafts only. Never post, send, publish or email. Jo pastes the draft.
- Rotate topics; read the last few drafts and the deep-dive delta notes so you do not
  repeat yourself.
