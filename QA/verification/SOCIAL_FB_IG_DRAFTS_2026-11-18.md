## Facebook (link allowed, in post body)

Letting AI search read your site and letting AI models train on it are two different choices.

Most robots.txt files treat them as one. A blanket block can shut out the crawlers that fetch pages for search and answer results. A blanket allow can open the door to training you never decided on.

Worth doing now, whichever way you lean:
- Read your robots.txt and note which bots are named
- Keep search crawlers and training bots separate, in your head and in the file
- Being allowed at the root is crawl readiness only. It is not a ranking or a mention

Our robots.txt checker shows how your rules read for search, training and user-triggered bots. It is informational and does not prove indexing:
