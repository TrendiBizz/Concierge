# Competitor facts — Goodcall, Smith.ai, Allo (primary sources only)

Fetched 2026-11-25 from vendor pages (web-fetch agent, which summarises pages; quotes are as extracted).
**[V]** = stated on a vendor page named below. Currency is shown only as "$" on all three sites; the pages
say "$", not "USD", so Trendi pages must say "$" for rivals. Third-party reviews were not used.

## Goodcall (https://www.goodcall.com/pricing, /, /how-it-works)
- [V] Starter "$79 a month": "100 unique customers a month then 79¢ each", about 150 calls/month, 1 team member, 30-day call history.
- [V] Growth "$129 a month": 500 unique customers then 26¢ each, about 750 calls/month, 5 team members.
- [V] Scale "$299 a month": 2,000 unique customers then 15¢ each, about 3,000 calls/month, 10 team members.
- [V] Enterprise: contact sales, no price. "No per-minute charges." "We never meter minutes."
- [V] Free demo agent: "only pay when you connect a number". No trial length stated.
- [V] Every plan includes a local number. Forwarding from your existing number, guides for 24 carriers (how-it-works). The word "porting" does not appear.
- [V] Hands off to a person, next person on list up to three (how-it-works). Calendar: Google Calendar; leads to Google Sheet or HighLevel; API/MCP tools.
- [V] Languages: "English, today." Callers are told they are speaking with an AI assistant (home). SOC 2 and HIPAA compliant (home).
- NOT FOUND: contract, cancellation, refund terms; annual pricing; setup fees.

## Smith.ai (https://smith.ai/pricing, /pricing/ai-receptionist, /ai-receptionist, /receptionists, /)
- [V] AI Receptionist: Free "$0/mo", 25 calls, then $3.00/call, card required. Pro "$150/mo", 75 calls, $2.00/call after. Enterprise "$500/mo", 300 calls, $1.67/call.
- [V] Human Virtual Receptionist: Starter $300/mo 30 calls ($11.50 over); Basic $810/mo 90 calls ($10.50 over); Pro $2,100/mo 300 calls ($8.50 over). Answered by a real person 24/7.
- [V] Billed per call, not per minute. Month-to-month. AI plans: 30 days' notice to cancel. Human plans: 30-day money-back guarantee, 10% off for 12 months.
- [V] Warm transfer to a live North America-based receptionist; English and Spanish; Clio, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier.
- DISCREPANCIES on vendor pages (do not cite): human escalation fee ($3 on home vs no fee when AI-initiated); refund window (two weeks vs 30 days); spam billing; integration count (5,000+ vs 7,000+).
- NOT FOUND: hybrid monthly prices; live-agent add-on price on AI plans; money-back guarantee on AI plans.

## Allo (https://www.withallo.com/pricing, /, /product/ai-receptionist)
- [V] Business "$32 a seat a month billed yearly (or $45 monthly)". Ultra $100 yearly / $120 monthly, through sales. Pricing page says "Pricing is early and can still change." Price display on the page was garbled; figures are from the FAQ text.
- [V] AI Receptionist add-on "$32 a month per agent". Extra numbers from $5/month. Power Dialer $50, HIPAA BAA $50.
- [V] 3-day free trial, card on file, first 30 days money-back. "No setup fees and no contracts." Cancel anytime. Guided setup is a one-time fee, amount not stated.
- [V] Numbers in 80+ countries, "free porting", unlimited national calls, inbound SMS unlimited. AI receptionist answers day and night, English/French/Spanish, transfers to a human, answers calls but does not place them.
- [V] Integrations: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Attio, GoHighLevel, Odoo, Google Sheets; Zapier/Make/API.
- NOT FOUND: included minutes or AI call limits; AI overage pricing; terms of service wording.
