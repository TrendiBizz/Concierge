# Privacy Act 2020 surface: statement, deletion, consent

Concierge stores enquiries, messages, call recordings and transcripts. Three things the Act requires
of a business like this were missing from the product: a reachable statement, a real path to deletion,
and any record of consent per contact.

## What exists now

* **`/privacy` on the product host** (was a 404). States what is held and why, links the full statement
  at `https://trendibizz.com/privacy.html`, and carries a form to ask what is held or have it deleted.
* **`/privacy/request`** records the request against every business that holds that email (or as a
  platform-level request if none does), and notifies the owner through the outbox — recorded, retried,
  visible in Studio. It is never silently absorbed.
* **`GET /owner/privacy/requests`** gives the business the queue; **`POST /owner/privacy/requests/{id}/erase`**
  performs the deletion: the lead row is kept for the business's records and audit but the person is
  removed — name, email, phone, message and company blanked, `erased_at` set, enquiry source text
  deleted, conversation messages deleted. The request is marked done with who did it.
* **`POST /owner/privacy/contacts/{key}/consent`** and the `contact_consents` table record, per contact
  per channel, whether they agreed, on what source (e.g. "customer replied STOP"), when.
* **A Privacy link in the Studio header**, next to "Powered by Trendi".

## Stripe: what is actually there

There is **no Stripe integration in the product** — no key, no SDK, no Stripe API call anywhere in
`api/` and no Stripe environment variable on the VPS or in any container. What the business does use is
**Stripe Payment Links** on the marketing site (`buy.stripe.com/...` in `pricing.html`) to take
subscription and deposit money for Trendi's own services, plus the Stripe customer-portal link
(`billing.stripe.com/...`). Stripe cannot invoice a tradesman's customers without an integration, so
invoices are generated by us — and an invoice now carries a **payment link** so a customer can pay by
card through Stripe (or any https checkout): `POST /owner/invoices/{id}/payment-link`, shown on the
invoice as "Pay … now". Links must be `https://`.
