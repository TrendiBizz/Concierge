Jo asked for one place where customers find setup help: the 7 stage videos plus the onboarding path, reachable from a "Need help?" banner. Videos were only on /guide and the Studio hub.

- Footer "Help and onboarding" link in the Explore nav on every page that has it.
- Studio Setup Hub: "Full walkthrough" link became "Help and onboarding" -> https://trendibizz.com/help.
- Registered: tools/a2ui/pages.json, sitemap (`site_seo.py sitemap --write`), llms.txt.
- YouTube titles/descriptions for the 7 videos: argo-video/out/youtube-help-videos.txt (not in repo; Jo uploads).
